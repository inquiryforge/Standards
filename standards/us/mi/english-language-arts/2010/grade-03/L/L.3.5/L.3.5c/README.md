# L.3.5c - Distinguish shades of meaning among related words that describe states of mind or...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Distinguish shades of meaning among related words that describe states of mind or degrees of certainty (e.g., knew, believed, suspected, heard, wondered).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
