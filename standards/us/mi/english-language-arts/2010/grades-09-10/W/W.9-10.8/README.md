# W.9-10.8 - Gather relevant information from multiple authoritative print and digital sources,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Gather relevant information from multiple authoritative print and digital sources, using advanced searches effectively; assess the usefulness of each source in answering the research question; integrate information into the text selectively to maintain the flow of ideas, avoiding plagiarism and following a standard format for citation. research to Build and Present Knowledge (continued)
