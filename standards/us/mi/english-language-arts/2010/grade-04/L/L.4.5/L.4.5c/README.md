# L.4.5c - Demonstrate understanding of words by relating them to their opposites (antonyms)...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate understanding of words by relating them to their opposites (antonyms) and to words with similar but not identical meanings (synonyms).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
