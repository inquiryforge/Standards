# RI.7.1 - Cite several pieces of textual evidence to support 1 analysis of what the text says...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Cite several pieces of textual evidence to support 1 analysis of what the text says explicitly as well as inferences drawn from the text.
