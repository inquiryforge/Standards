# RI.7.8 - Trace and evaluate the argument and specific 8 claims in a text, assessing whether...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Trace and evaluate the argument and specific 8 claims in a text, assessing whether the reasoning is sound and the evidence is relevant and sufficient to support the claims.
