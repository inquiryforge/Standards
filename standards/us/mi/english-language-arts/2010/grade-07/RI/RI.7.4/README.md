# RI.7.4 - Determine the meaning of words and phrases 4 as they are used in a text, including...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Determine the meaning of words and phrases 4 as they are used in a text, including figurative, connotative, and technical meanings; analyze the impact of a specific word choice on meaning and tone.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
