# RI.7.10 - By the end of the year, read and comprehend 1 literary nonfiction in the grades 6–8...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
By the end of the year, read and comprehend 1 literary nonfiction in the grades 6–8 text complexity band proficiently, with scaffolding as needed at the high end of the range.
