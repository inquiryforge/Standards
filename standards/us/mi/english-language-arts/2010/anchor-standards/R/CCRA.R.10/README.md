# CCRA.R.10 - Read and comprehend complex literary and informational texts independently and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Read and comprehend complex literary and informational texts independently and proficiently. Please see “Research to Build and Present Knowledge” in Writing and “Comprehension and Collaboration” in Speaking and Listening for additional standards relevant to gathering, assessing, and applying information from print and digital sources.
