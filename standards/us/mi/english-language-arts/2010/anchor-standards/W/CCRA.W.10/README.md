# CCRA.W.10 - Write routinely over extended time frames (time for research, reflection, and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Write routinely over extended time frames (time for research, reflection, and revision) and shorter time frames (a single sitting or a day or two) for a range of tasks, purposes, and audiences. These broad types of writing include many subgenres. See Appendix A for definitions of key writing types.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
