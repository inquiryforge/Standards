# L.2.5b - Distinguish shades of meaning among closely related verbs (e.g., toss, throw, hurl)...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Distinguish shades of meaning among closely related verbs (e.g., toss, throw, hurl) and closely related adjectives (e.g., thin, slender, skinny, scrawny).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
