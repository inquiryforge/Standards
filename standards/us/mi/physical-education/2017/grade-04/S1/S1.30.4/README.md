# S1.30.4 - Demonstrate above water arm recovery and rudimentary kick with face in the water,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate above water arm recovery and rudimentary kick with face in the water, breathing to the side to swim 15 feet/5 yards in the water in isolated settings.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
