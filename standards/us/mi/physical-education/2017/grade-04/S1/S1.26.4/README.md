# S1.26.4 - Combines traveling with manipulative skills of dribbling, throwing, catching and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Combines traveling with manipulative skills of dribbling, throwing, catching and striking in teacher- and/or student-designed small-sided practice-task environments.
