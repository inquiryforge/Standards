# S1.19.4 - a. Passes and receives ball with the insides of the feet to a moving partner in a...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
a. Passes and receives ball with the insides of the feet to a moving partner in a nondynamic environment (closed skills). b. Receives and passes a ball with the outsides and insides of the feet to a stationary partner, “giving” on reception before returning the pass.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
