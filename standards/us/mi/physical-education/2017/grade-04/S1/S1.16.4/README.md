# S1.16.4 - Catches a thrown ball above the head, at chest or waist level, and below the waist...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Catches a thrown ball above the head, at chest or waist level, and below the waist using a mature pattern in a nondynamic environment (closed environment).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
