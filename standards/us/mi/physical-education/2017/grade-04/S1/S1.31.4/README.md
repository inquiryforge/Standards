# S1.31.4 - Demonstrate locomotion, in a supine position, using above water arm recovery, with...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate locomotion, in a supine position, using above water arm recovery, with arms entering at or above shoulder level with continuous kick 15 feet/5 yards in the water in isolated settings.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
