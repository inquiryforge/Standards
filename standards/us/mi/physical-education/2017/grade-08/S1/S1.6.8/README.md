# S1.6.8 - Executes at least 2 of the following to create open space during modified game play:...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Executes at least 2 of the following to create open space during modified game play: pivots, fakes, jab steps, screens.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
