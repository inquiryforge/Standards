# S3.6.8 - Participates in moderate to vigorous aerobic and/or muscle- and bone-strengthening...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Participates in moderate to vigorous aerobic and/or muscle- and bone-strengthening physical activity for at least 60 minutes per day at least 5 times per week.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
