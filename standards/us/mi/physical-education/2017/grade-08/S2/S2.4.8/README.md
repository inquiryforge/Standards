# S2.4.8 - Reduces open space on defense by staying on the goal side of the offensive player...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Reduces open space on defense by staying on the goal side of the offensive player and reducing the distance to him/her (third-party perspective).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
