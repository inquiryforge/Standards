# S1.30.5 - Demonstrate above water arm recovery and continuous kick with face in the water,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate above water arm recovery and continuous kick with face in the water, breathing consistently to the side to swim 15 yards in the water in isolated settings.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
