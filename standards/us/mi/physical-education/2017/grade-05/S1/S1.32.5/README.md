# S1.32.5 - Demonstrate jumping or stepping from the side while wearing a life jacket,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate jumping or stepping from the side while wearing a life jacket, demonstrate help and huddle positions for one minute, and locomote to a position of safety in controlled settings.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
