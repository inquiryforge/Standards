# S5.6.6 - Demonstrates respect for self and others in activities and games by following the...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrates respect for self and others in activities and games by following the rules, encouraging others, and playing in the spirit of the game or activity.
