# S1.2.L1 - Demonstrates competency in dance forms used in cultural and social occasions (e.g....

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrates competency in dance forms used in cultural and social occasions (e.g. weddings, parties) or demonstrates competency in 1 form of dance (e.g. ballet, modern, hip hop, tap).
