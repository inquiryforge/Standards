# S3.6.L2 - Creates a plan, trains for, and participates in a community event with a focus on...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Creates a plan, trains for, and participates in a community event with a focus on physical activity (e.g. 5K, triathlon, tournament, dance performance, cycling event).
