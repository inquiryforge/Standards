# P3.1.3 - Give examples of how conflicts over Democratic Values lead people to differ on...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Give examples of how conflicts over Democratic Values lead people to differ on resolutions to a public policy issue in Michigan.

Examples may include but are not limited to: common good, equality, individual rights, justice (fairness).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
