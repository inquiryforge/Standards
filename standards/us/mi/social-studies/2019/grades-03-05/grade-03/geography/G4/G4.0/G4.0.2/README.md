# G4.0.2 - Describe diverse groups that have migrated into a region of Michigan and reasons...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe diverse groups that have migrated into a region of Michigan and reasons why they came (push/pull factors).

Examples may include but are not limited to: Finnish migrating to the upper peninsula, Chaldeans migrating into southeastern Michigan, Dutch migrating to western Michigan.
