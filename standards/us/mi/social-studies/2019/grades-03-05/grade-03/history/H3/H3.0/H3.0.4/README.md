# H3.0.4 - Draw upon traditional stories and/or teachings of Indigenous Peoples who lived and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Draw upon traditional stories and/or teachings of Indigenous Peoples who lived and continue to live in Michigan in order to better understand their beliefs and histories.

Examples may include but are not limited to: Teachings of the Seven Grandfathers.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
