# H3.0.1 - Identify questions historians ask in examining the past in Michigan.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify questions historians ask in examining the past in Michigan.

Examples may include but are not limited to: What happened? When did it happen? Who was involved? How and why did it happen?
