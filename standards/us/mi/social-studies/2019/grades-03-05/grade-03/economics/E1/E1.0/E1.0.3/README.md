# E1.0.3 - Analyze how Michigan’s location and natural resources influenced its economic...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Analyze how Michigan’s location and natural resources influenced its economic development.

Examples may include but are not limited to: how waterways and other natural resources have influenced economic activities such as farming, mining, lumbering, automobile manufacturing, and furniture making.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
