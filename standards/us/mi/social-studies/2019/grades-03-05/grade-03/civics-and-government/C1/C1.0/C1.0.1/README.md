# C1.0.1 - Give an example of how Michigan state government fulfills one of the purposes of...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Give an example of how Michigan state government fulfills one of the purposes of government.

Examples may include but are not limited to: protecting individual rights, promoting the common good, ensuring equal treatment under the law.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
