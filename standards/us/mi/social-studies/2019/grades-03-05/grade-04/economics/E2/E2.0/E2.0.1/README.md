# E2.0.1 - Explain how changes in the United States economy impact levels of employment and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain how changes in the United States economy impact levels of employment and unemployment.

Examples may include but are not limited to: changing demand for natural resources, changes in technology, changes in competition.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
