# E1.0.2 - Describe characteristics of a market economy.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe characteristics of a market economy.

Examples may include but are not limited to: private property rights, voluntary exchange, competition, consumer sovereignty, incentives, specialization.
