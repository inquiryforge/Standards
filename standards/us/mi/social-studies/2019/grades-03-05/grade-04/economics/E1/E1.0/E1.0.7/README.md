# E1.0.7 - Describe the role of money in the exchange of goods and services.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe the role of money in the exchange of goods and services.

Examples may include but are not limited to: people earn income and use the income to purchase goods and services.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
