# E1.0.6 - Explain how competition among buyers results in higher prices, and competition...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain how competition among buyers results in higher prices, and competition among sellers results in lower prices.

Examples may include but are not limited to: supply, demand.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
