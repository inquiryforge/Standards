# H3.0.6 - Use a variety of primary and secondary sources to construct a historical narrative...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Use a variety of primary and secondary sources to construct a historical narrative about the beginnings of the automobile industry and the labor movement in Michigan.

Examples may include but are not limited to: stories, photos, artifacts, oral history, letters.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
