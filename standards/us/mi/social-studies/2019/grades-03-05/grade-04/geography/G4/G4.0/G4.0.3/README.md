# G4.0.3 - Describe some of the movements of resources, goods, people, and information to,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe some of the movements of resources, goods, people, and information to, from, or within the United States, and explain the reasons for the movements.

Examples may include but are not limited to: movement of fossil fuels, clothing, retirees, refugees, migrant farm workers, and manufacturing jobs into and within the United States.
