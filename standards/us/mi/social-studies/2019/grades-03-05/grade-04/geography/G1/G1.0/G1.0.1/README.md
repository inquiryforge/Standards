# G1.0.1 - Identify questions geographers ask in examining the United States.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify questions geographers ask in examining the United States.

Examples may include but are not limited to: Where is it? What is it like there? How is it connected to other places?
<!-- standard-text:end -->
