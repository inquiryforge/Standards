# G1.0.2 - Identify and describe the characteristics and purposes of a variety of...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify and describe the characteristics and purposes of a variety of technological geographic tools.

Examples of purposes may include but are not limited to: measure distance, determine relative or absolute location, classify a region.

Examples of tools and technologies may include but are not limited to: globe, map, Geographic Information Systems (GIS), satellite image.
