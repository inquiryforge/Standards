# G2.0.1 - Describe ways in which the United States can be divided into different regions.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe ways in which the United States can be divided into different regions.

Examples may include but are not limited to: political regions, land-use regions, land-form regions, vegetation regions.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
