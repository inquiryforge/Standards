# C3.0.2 - Give examples of powers exercised by the federal government, tribal governments...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Give examples of powers exercised by the federal government, tribal governments and state governments.

Examples for federal government may include but are not limited to: coining of money, declaring war.

Examples for tribal governments may include but are not limited to: issuing hunting, gathering, and fishing licenses, issuing tribal identification cards.

Examples for state governments may include but are not limited to: issuing driver’s licenses, issuing marriage licenses.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
