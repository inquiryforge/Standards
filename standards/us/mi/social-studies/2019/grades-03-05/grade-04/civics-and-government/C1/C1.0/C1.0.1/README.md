# C1.0.1 - Identify questions political scientists ask in examining the United States.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify questions political scientists ask in examining the United States.

Examples may include but are not limited to: What does government do? What are the basic values and principles of American democracy? What are the roles of the citizen in American democracy?
<!-- standard-text:end -->
