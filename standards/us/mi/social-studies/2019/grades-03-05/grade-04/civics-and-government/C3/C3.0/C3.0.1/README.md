# C3.0.1 - Give examples of ways the Constitution limits the powers of the federal government.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Give examples of ways the Constitution limits the powers of the federal government.

Examples may include but are not limited to: election of public officers, separation of powers, checks and balances, Bill of Rights.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
