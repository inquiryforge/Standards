# C3.0.5 - Give examples of how the system of checks and balances limits the power of the...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Give examples of how the system of checks and balances limits the power of the federal government.

Examples may include but are not limited to: presidential veto of legislation, courts declaring a law unconstitutional, congressional approval of judicial appointments.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
