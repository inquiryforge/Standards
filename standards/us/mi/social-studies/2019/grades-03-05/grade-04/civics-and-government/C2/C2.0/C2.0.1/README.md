# C2.0.1 - Explain how the principles of popular sovereignty, rule of law, checks and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain how the principles of popular sovereignty, rule of law, checks and balances, separation of powers, and individual rights serve to limit the powers of the federal government as reflected in the Constitution and Bill of Rights.

Examples may include but are not limited to: individual rights (e.g., freedom of religion, freedom of expression, and freedom of press).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
