# U2 - USHG ERA 2 – COLONIZATION AND SETTLEMENT (1585-1763)

[Parent](../README.md)

## Source text

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Individually and collaboratively, students will engage in planned inquiries to understand how European values and institutions transferred to and modified in the colonies, and how slavery reshaped European and African life in the Americas.
