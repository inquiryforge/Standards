# U2.2.3 - Describe how enslaved and free Africans struggled to retain elements of their...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe how enslaved and free Africans struggled to retain elements of their diverse African histories and cultures to develop distinct African-American identities.

Examples may include but are not limited to: Gullah Islands, Louisiana, The Carolinas.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
