# U2.3.4 - Describe the development of the emerging labor force in the colonies.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe the development of the emerging labor force in the colonies.

Examples may include but are not limited to: cash-crop farming, slavery, indentured servants.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
