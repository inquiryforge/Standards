# U2.3.3 - Describe colonial life in America from the perspectives of at least three...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe colonial life in America from the perspectives of at least three different groups of people.

Examples may include but are not limited to: perspectives of wealthy landowners, farmers, merchants, indentured servants, laborers, the poor, women, enslaved people, free Africans, and Indigenous Peoples.
