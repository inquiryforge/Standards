# U2.2.1 - Describe Triangular Trade, including:

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe Triangular Trade, including:

- the trade routes.

- the people and goods that were traded.

- the Middle Passage.

- the impact on life in Africa.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
