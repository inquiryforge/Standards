# U3.3.5 - Give reasons why the Framers wanted to limit the power of government.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Give reasons why the Framers wanted to limit the power of government.

Examples may include but are not limited to: fear of a strong executive, representative government, and the importance of individual rights.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
