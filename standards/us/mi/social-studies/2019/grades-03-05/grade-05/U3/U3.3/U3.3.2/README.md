# U3.3.2 - Give examples of problems the country faced under the Articles of Confederation.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Give examples of problems the country faced under the Articles of Confederation.

Examples may include but are not limited to: lack of national army, competing currencies, reliance on state governments for money.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
