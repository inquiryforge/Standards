# U1.2.1 - Explain the technological and political developments that made sea exploration...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain the technological and political developments that made sea exploration possible.

Examples may include but are not limited to: the invention of the astrolabe, improved maps, the rise of nation-states.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
