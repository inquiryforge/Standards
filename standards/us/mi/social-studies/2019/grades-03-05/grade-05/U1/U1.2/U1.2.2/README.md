# U1.2.2 - Use case studies of individual explorers and stories of life in Europe to compare...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Use case studies of individual explorers and stories of life in Europe to compare the goals, obstacles, motivations, and consequences for European exploration and colonization of the Americas.

Examples may include but are not limited to: the economic, political, cultural, and religious consequences of colonization, including who was impacted.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
