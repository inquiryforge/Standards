# H2.0.5 - Describe how community members responded to a problem in the past.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe how community members responded to a problem in the past.

Examples may include but are not limited to: natural disasters, factories closing, poverty, homelessness, closing of military bases, environmental issues.
