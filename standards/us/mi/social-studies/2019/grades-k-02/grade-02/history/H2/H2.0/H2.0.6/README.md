# H2.0.6 - Construct a historical narrative about the history of the local community from a...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Construct a historical narrative about the history of the local community from a variety of sources.

Examples may include but are not limited to: data gathered from local residents, artifacts, photographs.
