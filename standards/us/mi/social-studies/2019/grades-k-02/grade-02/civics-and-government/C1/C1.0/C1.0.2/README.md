# C1.0.2 - Distinguish between government action and private action.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Distinguish between government action and private action.

Examples may include but are not limited to: city snowplows clearing roads (government action), clearing the snow on your sidewalk or driveway (private action).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
