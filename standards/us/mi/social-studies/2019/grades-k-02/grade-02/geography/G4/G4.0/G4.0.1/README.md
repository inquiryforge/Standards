# G4.0.1 - Describe land use in the community.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe land use in the community.

Examples may include but are not limited to: where people live, where services are provided, where products are made, where people play, where people interact with the land.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
