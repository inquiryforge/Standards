# C1.0.2 - Give examples of the use of power with authority and power without authority in...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Give examples of the use of power with authority and power without authority in school.

Examples may include but are not limited to: principal, teacher, bus driver, line leader of safety patrol (power with authority), types of bullying, taking cuts in line (power without authority).
