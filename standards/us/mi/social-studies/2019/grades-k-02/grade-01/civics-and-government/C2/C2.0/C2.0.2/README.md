# C2.0.2 - Identify important symbols of the United States of America and what they represent.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify important symbols of the United States of America and what they represent.

Examples may include but are not limited to: the U.S. flag, Statue of Liberty, White House, Bald Eagle.
