# C5.0.2 - Explain important rights and how, when, and where members of American society...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain important rights and how, when, and where members of American society demonstrate their responsibilities by actively participating in civic life.

Examples may include but are not limited to: cleaning the playground, helping others, helping solve a problem, respecting the rights of others.
