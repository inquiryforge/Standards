# C2.0.1 - Explain fair ways to make decisions and resolve conflicts in the school community.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain fair ways to make decisions and resolve conflicts in the school community.

Examples may include but are not limited to: majority rules, taking turns, voting, talking it out, referring to an authority.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
