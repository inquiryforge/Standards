# C5.0.1 - Describe some responsibilities people have at home and at school.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe some responsibilities people have at home and at school.

Examples may include but are not limited to: taking care of oneself, respect for the rights of others, following rules, getting along with others.
