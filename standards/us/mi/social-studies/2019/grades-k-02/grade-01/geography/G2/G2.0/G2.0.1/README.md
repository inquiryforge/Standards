# G2.0.1 - Distinguish between physical and human characteristics of places.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Distinguish between physical and human characteristics of places.

Examples may include but are not limited to: trees, landmasses, bodies of water (physical/natural), buildings, playgrounds, sidewalks, roads (human).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
