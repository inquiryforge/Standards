# G1.0.3 - Distinguish between landmasses and bodies of water using maps and globes.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Distinguish between landmasses and bodies of water using maps and globes.

Examples may include but are not limited to: islands and continents (landmasses), rivers, lakes, oceans (bodies of water).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
