# H2.0.1 - Demonstrate chronological thinking by distinguishing among past, present, and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate chronological thinking by distinguishing among past, present, and future using family or school events.

Examples may include but are not limited to: using a calendar to distinguish among days, weeks, and months.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
