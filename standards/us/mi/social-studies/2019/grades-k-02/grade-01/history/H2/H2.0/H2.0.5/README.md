# H2.0.5 - Identify the events or people celebrated during U.S. national holidays and why we...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify the events or people celebrated during U.S. national holidays and why we celebrate them.

Examples may include but are not limited to: Independence Day, Constitution Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents Day, Veterans Day.
