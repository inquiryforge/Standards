# C2.0.2 - Explain why people do not have the right to do whatever they want.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain why people do not have the right to do whatever they want.

Examples may include but are not limited to: promote fairness, ensure the common good, maintain safety.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
