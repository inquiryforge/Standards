# G1.0.2 - Use directions or positional words to identify significant locations in the classroom.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Use directions or positional words to identify significant locations in the classroom.

Examples may include but are not limited to: up/down, in/out, above/below, left/right.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
