# 6.1.3 - Urbanization

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Urbanization – explain the causes and consequences of urbanization, including:

- the location and expansion of major urban centers and their link to industry and trade.

- internal migration, including the Great Migration.

- the development of cities divided by race, ethnicity, and class, as well as the resulting tensions among and within groups.

- different perspectives about the immigrant experience.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
