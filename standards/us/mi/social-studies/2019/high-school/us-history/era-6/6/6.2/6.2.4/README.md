# 6.2.4 - Wilson and His Opponents

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Wilson and His Opponents – explain how President Woodrow Wilson’s “Fourteen Points” differed from proposals by others, including French and British leaders and domestic opponents, in the debate over:

- the Treaty of Versailles.

- U.S. participation in the League of Nations.

- the redrawing of European political boundaries and the resulting geopolitical tensions that continued to affect Europe.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
