# 7.2.1 - Causes of World War II

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Causes of World War II – analyze the factors contributing to World War II in Europe and in the Pacific region, and America’s entry into war, including:

- political and economic disputes over territory.

- the differences in the civic and political values of the United States and those of Nazi Germany and Imperial Japan.

- U.S. neutrality.

- the bombing of Pearl Harbor.

Examples may include but are not limited to: failure of the Treaty of Versailles; the League of Nations; the Munich Agreement; the Neutrality Acts; the Lend Lease Act; oil embargo; fascism; militarism, nationalism; imperialism.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
