# 7.2.4 - Responses to Genocide

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Responses to Genocide – investigate the responses to Hitler’s “Final Solution” policy by the Allies, the U.S. government, international organizations, and individuals.

Examples may include but are not limited to: concentration camp liberation, Nuremberg war crimes tribunals, and actions by individuals such as Oskar Schindler and Irena Sendler as examples of the “righteous among the nations”.
