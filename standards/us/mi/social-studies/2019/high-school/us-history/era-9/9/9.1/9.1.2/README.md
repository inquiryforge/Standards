# 9.1.2 - Transformation of American Politics

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Transformation of American Politics – analyze the transformation of American politics in the late 20th and early 21st centuries, including:

- the growth of the conservative movement in national politics, including the role of Ronald Reagan.

- the role of evangelical religion in national politics.

- the intensification of partisanship.

- the partisan conflict over the role of government in American life.

- the role of regional differences in national politics.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
