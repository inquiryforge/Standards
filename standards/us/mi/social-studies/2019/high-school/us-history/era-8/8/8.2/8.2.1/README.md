# 8.2.1 - Demographic Changes

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demographic Changes – use population data to produce and analyze maps that show the major changes in population distribution and spatial patterns and density, including the Baby Boom, new immigration, suburbanization, reverse migration of African-Americans to the South, the Indian Relocation Act of 1956, and the flow of population to the Sunbelt.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
