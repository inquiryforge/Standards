# 8.1.1 - Origins and Beginnings of the Cold War

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Origins and Beginnings of the Cold War – analyze the factors that contributed to the Cold War, including:

- differences in the civic, ideological, and political values, and in the economic and governmental institutions, of the United States and the Soviet Union (U.S.S.R.).

- diplomatic and political actions by both the United States and the U.S.S.R. in the last years of World War II and the years afterward.

Examples may include but are not limited to: the differences between Communism and Capitalism, diplomatic decisions made at the Yalta and Potsdam conferences, the use of the atomic bomb, the Marshall Plan, Truman Doctrine, United Nations, North American Treaty Organization (NATO), and the Warsaw Pact.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
