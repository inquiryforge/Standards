# 1.4.4 - Market Failure

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Market Failure – explain the role for government in addressing both negative and positive externalities.

Examples may include but are not limited to: pollution, vaccinations, education, medical research, government/private partnerships.
