# 1.3.1 - Supply And Demand

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Supply And Demand – use the laws of supply and demand to explain household and business behavior.

Examples may include but are not limited to: determinants of demand and determinants of supply.
