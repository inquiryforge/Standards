# 1.2.2 - Market Structures

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Market Structures – identify the characteristics of perfect competition,monopolistic competition, oligopoly, and monopoly market structures.

Examples may include but are not limited to: number of producers, similarity of products, barriers to entry, control over prices.
