# 1.2.1 - Institutions

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Institutions – describe the roles of various economic institutions and purposes they serve in a market economy.

Examples may include but are not limited to: banks, labor unions, markets, corporations, co-operatives, sole proprietorships, partnerships, and not-for-profit organizations.
