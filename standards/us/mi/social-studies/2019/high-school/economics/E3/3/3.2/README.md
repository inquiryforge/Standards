# 3.2 - Economic Interdependence – Trade

[Parent](../README.md)

## Source text

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Individually and collaboratively, students will engage in planned inquiries to describe how trade generates economic development and interdependence, and analyze the benefits and costs for individuals, producers, and governments.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
