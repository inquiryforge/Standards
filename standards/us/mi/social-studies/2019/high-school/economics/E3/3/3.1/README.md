# 3.1 - Economic Systems

[Parent](../README.md)

## Source text

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Individually and collaboratively, students will engage in planned inquiries to explain how different economic systems, including free market, command, and mixed systems, coordinate and facilitate the exchange, production, distribution, and consumption of goods and services.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
