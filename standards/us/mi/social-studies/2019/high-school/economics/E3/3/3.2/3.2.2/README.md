# 3.2.2 - Domestic Activity and World Trade

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Domestic Activity and World Trade – assess the impact of trade policies, monetary policy, exchange rates, and interest rates on domestic activity and world trade.

Examples may include but are not limited to: tariffs, quotas, export subsidies, product standards, other barriers.
