# 2.1 - Understanding National Markets

[Parent](../README.md)

## Source text

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Individually and collaboratively, students will engage in planned inquiries to explain why inflation, unemployment, output, and growth in potential output matter to consumers and producers, as well as associate stable money and interest rates with economic prosperity.
