# FOUNDATIONS OF HIGH SCHOOL WORLD HISTORY AND GEOGRAPHY

[Parent](../README.md)

## Source text

<!-- standard-text:start -->
How do world historians make sense of a global past? Why are geography and spatial reasoning important for understanding world history? These foundational expectations help students answer such questions, and introduce them to the tools they will need to study world history. Individually and collaboratively, students can engage in planned inquiries.
