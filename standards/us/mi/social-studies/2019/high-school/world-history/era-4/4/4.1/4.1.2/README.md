# 4.1.2 - Intensifying Trade Networks and Contacts

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Intensifying Trade Networks and Contacts – compare and contrast the development, interdependence, specialization, and importance of interregional land-based and sea-based trading systems both within and between societies.

Examples may include but are not limited to: trans-Saharan trading in gold and salt; intensification of trade around the Indian Ocean; increasing trade and the growth of towns and cities in western Europe; the spread of the plague and significance of its consequences; networks of exchange in North, Central, and South America.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
