# 7.2.6 - Case Studies of Genocide

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Case Studies of Genocide – analyze the development, enactment, and consequences of, as well as the international community’s responses to, the Holocaust (or Shoah), Armenian Genocide, and at least one other genocide.

Examples may include but are not limited to: investigating the ideology and policies that led to genocide; policies to address and prevent genocide; cases studies of genocides such as Herero and Namaqua, Cambodia, Rwanda, Ukraine, and/or Bosnia.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
