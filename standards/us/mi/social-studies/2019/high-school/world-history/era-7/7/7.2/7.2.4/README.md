# 7.2.4 - Cold War Conflicts

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Cold War Conflicts – analyze the causes and consequences of major Cold War conflicts, including the global reconfigurations and restructuring of political and economic relationships in the post-World War II era.

Examples may include but are not limited to: investigating economic, political, and military origins of the Cold War; arms race and space race; comparing and contrasting conflicts in Asia, Africa, and Central America; the significance of the Cold War as a 20th century event, including transitions from bipolar to multipolar center(s) of power.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
