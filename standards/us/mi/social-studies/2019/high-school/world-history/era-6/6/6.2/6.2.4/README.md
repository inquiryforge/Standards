# 6.2.4 - Imperialism

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Imperialism – analyze the political, economic, and social causes and consequences of imperialism in different regions.

Examples may include but are not limited to: case studies of Japan (Meiji Restoration), Qing China, India, Egypt, Ethiopia and/or the Congo; encounters between imperial powers (Europe, Japan) and local people in India, Africa, Central Asia, and East Asia; the connection between imperialism and racism, including the social construction of race.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
