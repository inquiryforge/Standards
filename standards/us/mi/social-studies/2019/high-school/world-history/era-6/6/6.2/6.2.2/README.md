# 6.2.2 - Growth of Nationalism and Nation-States

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Growth of Nationalism and Nation-States – compare and contrast the rise of nation-states in a western and non-western context.

Examples may include but are not limited to: case studies of Germany, Italy, Japan.
