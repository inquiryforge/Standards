# CG4 - Conflict, Cooperation, and Security

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Analyze the causes and challenges of continuing and new conflicts by describing:

- tensions resulting from ethnic, territorial, religious, and/or nationalist differences.

- causes of and responses to ethnic cleansing/genocide/mass killing.

- local and global attempts at peacekeeping, security, democratization, and administration of international justice and human rights.

- the types of warfare used in these conflicts, including terrorism, private militias, and new technologies.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
