# P2.2 - Evaluate data presented in social science tables, graphs, graphics, maps, and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Evaluate data presented in social science tables, graphs, graphics, maps, and texts for credibility, considering the origin, authority, structure, and context of the information.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
