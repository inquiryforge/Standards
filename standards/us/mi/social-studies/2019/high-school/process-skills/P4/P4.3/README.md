# P4.3 - Plan, conduct, and evaluate the effectiveness of activities intended to advance...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Plan, conduct, and evaluate the effectiveness of activities intended to advance views on matters of public policy and to address local, regional, or global problems.
