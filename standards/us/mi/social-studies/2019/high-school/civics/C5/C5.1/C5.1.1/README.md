# C5.1.1 - Identify and describe ways in which foreign policy is made including...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify and describe ways in which foreign policy is made including Constitutional powers of the executive, legislative, and judicial branches and how those powers have been clarified or interpreted over time.

Examples may include but are not limited to: Senate treaty ratification powers, Senate advise and consent of political appointments, Congressional declarations of war, War Powers Act of 1973, executive orders and related injunctions, power of the purse.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
