# C5.1.3 - Describe ways in which groups and individuals influence foreign policy.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe ways in which groups and individuals influence foreign policy.

Examples may include but are not limited to: political action committees, voluntary organizations, professional organizations, civic organizations, media, individuals’ public opinions, interest groups, the media news cycles, think tanks, foreign policy.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
