# C5.2.4 - Identify and evaluate international non-governmental organizations.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify and evaluate international non-governmental organizations.

Examples may include but are not limited to: International Red Cross, Amnesty International, Doctors Without Borders.
