# C2.1.2 - Identify and analyze various Democratic Values of the United States as found in...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify and analyze various Democratic Values of the United States as found in the Declaration of Independence.

Examples of Democratic Values may include but are not limited to: justice, unalienable rights (life, liberty, pursuit of happiness), and equality.

Analysis may include but is not limited to: how might the ideals in the Declaration have been in tension with reality?
