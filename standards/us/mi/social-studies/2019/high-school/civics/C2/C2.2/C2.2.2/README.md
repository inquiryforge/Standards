# C2.2.2 - Analyze how influential historical speeches, writings, cases, and laws express...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Analyze how influential historical speeches, writings, cases, and laws express Democratic Values and influenced changes in American culture, law, and the Constitution.

Examples may include but are not limited to: equality; drawing upon Martin Luther King’s “I Have a Dream” speech and “Letter from Birmingham City Jail”; the Universal Declaration of Human Rights; the Declaration of Sentiments; the Equal Rights Amendment; and Dred Scott v. Sandford, Plessy v. Ferguson, Loving v. Virginia, the Americans With Disabilities Act, and Obergefell v. Hodges.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
