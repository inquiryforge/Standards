# C6.3.2 - Explain how informed members of society influence civic life.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain how informed members of society influence civic life.

Examples may include but are not limited to: obeying just law, disobeying unjust law, being informed and attentive to public issues, monitoring political leaders and governmental agencies, assuming leadership when appropriate, paying taxes, registering to vote and voting knowledgeably on candidates and issues, serving as a juror, serving in the armed forces, performing public service.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
