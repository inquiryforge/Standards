# C6.1.3 - Compare and contrast rights and representation among U.S. people and citizens...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Compare and contrast rights and representation among U.S. people and citizens living in states, territories, federal districts, and on tribally governed land.

Examples may include but are not limited to: District of Columbia, Guam, Puerto Rico, Northern Mariana Islands, U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa, Tribal Governments.
