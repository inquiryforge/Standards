# C6.3.1 - Explain the personal dispositions that contribute to knowledgeable and engaged...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain the personal dispositions that contribute to knowledgeable and engaged participation in civic communities.

Examples may include but are not limited to: concern for the well-being of others, civility, respect for the rights of other individuals, respect for law, honesty, open-mindedness, negotiation and compromise, persistence, civic-mindedness, compassion, patriotism, courage, and tolerance for ambiguity.
