# C6.4.3 - Identify and describe a local, state, national, or international public policy...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify and describe a local, state, national, or international public policy issue; research and evaluate multiple solutions; analyze the consequences of each solution and propose, defend, and take relevant action to address or resolve the issue.

Considerations for research may include but are not limited to: primary and secondary sources, legal documents (Constitutions, court decisions, state law), non-text based information (oral speeches/presentations, political cartoons, campaign advertisements), and other forms of political communication (speeches and blogs).

Considerations for analyzing credible sources may include but are not limited to: logical validity, factual accuracy and/or omission, emotional appeal, unstated assumptions, logical fallacies, inconsistencies, distortions, appeals to bias or prejudice, overall strength of argument.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
