# C6.4.4 - Equip students with the skills and knowledge to explore multiple pathways for...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Equip students with the skills and knowledge to explore multiple pathways for knowledgeable, civic engagement through simulations and/or real-world opportunities for involvement.

Examples may include but are not limited to: trials, school board meetings, congressional hearings, running for office, letters to the editor, political campaigns.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
