# C3.3.6 - Explain functions and possible influence of various news and other media sources...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain functions and possible influence of various news and other media sources in political communication.

Examples may include but are not limited to: television, print, press, Internet (including social media), radio.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
