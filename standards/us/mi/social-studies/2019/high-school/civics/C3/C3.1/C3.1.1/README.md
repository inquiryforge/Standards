# C3.1.1 - Identify and describe the purposes, organization, powers, processes, and election...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify and describe the purposes, organization, powers, processes, and election of the legislative branch as enumerated in Article I of the Constitution.

Examples may include but are not limited to: the House of Representatives and Senate (including election and qualifications to hold office), advise and consent, impeachment, power of the purse, approval of treaties, and war powers.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
