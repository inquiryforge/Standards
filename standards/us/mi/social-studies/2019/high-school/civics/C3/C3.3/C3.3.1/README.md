# C3.3.1 - Describe and analyze how groups and individuals influence public policy.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe and analyze how groups and individuals influence public policy.

Examples may include but are not limited to: political action committees, voluntary organizations, professional organizations, civic organizations, media.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
