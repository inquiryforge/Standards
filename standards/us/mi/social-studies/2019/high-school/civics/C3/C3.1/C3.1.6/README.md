# C3.1.6 - Evaluate major sources of revenue and major expenditures of the federal government.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Evaluate major sources of revenue and major expenditures of the federal government.

Examples may include but are not limited to: discretionary spending, federal income tax, and mandatory spending.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
