# C3.1.3 - Identify and describe the purposes, organization, powers, processes, and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify and describe the purposes, organization, powers, processes, and appointment or election of the judicial branch as enumerated in Article III of the Constitution and as established in Marbury v. Madison.

Examples may include but are not limited to: the Supreme Court (nomination and appointment process, lifetime tenure), original and appellate jurisdictions, resolution of disputes.
