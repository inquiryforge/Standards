# C1.1.2 - Identify, provide examples of, and distinguish among different systems of...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify, provide examples of, and distinguish among different systems of government by analyzing similarities and differences in sovereignty, power, legitimacy, and authority.

Examples may include but are not limited to: anarchy, dictatorship, democracy, monarchy, oligarchy, republic, theocracy, military junta, socialist, and tribal governments.
