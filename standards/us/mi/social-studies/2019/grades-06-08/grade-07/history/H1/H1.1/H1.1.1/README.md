# H1.1.1 - Compare and contrast several different calendar systems used in the past and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Compare and contrast several different calendar systems used in the past and present and their cultural significance.

Examples may include but are not limited to: sundial; lunar solar; Gregorian calendar: BC/AD; contemporary secular: BCE/CE; Chinese; Hebrew; Islamic/Hijri.
