# H1.2.1 - Explain how historians use a variety of sources to explore the past.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain how historians use a variety of sources to explore the past.

Examples may include but are not limited to: artifacts, primary and secondary sources including narratives, technology, historical maps, visual/mathematical quantitative data, radiocarbon dating, and DNA analysis.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
