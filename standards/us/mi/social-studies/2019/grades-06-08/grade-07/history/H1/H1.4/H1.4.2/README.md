# H1.4.2 - Describe and use themes of history to study patterns of change and continuity.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe and use themes of history to study patterns of change and continuity.

Examples may include but are not limited to: several lists of history themes are available, including: SPEC (social, political, economic, and cultural) and the World History For Us All1 themes (Patterns of Population; Economic Networks and Exchange; Uses and Abuses of Power; Haves and Have-Nots; Expressing Identity; Science, Technology, and the Environment; and Spiritual Life and Moral Codes).
