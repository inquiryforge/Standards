# G1.2.2 - Apply the skills of geographic inquiry (asking geographic questions, acquiring...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Apply the skills of geographic inquiry (asking geographic questions, acquiring geographic information, organizing geographic information, analyzing geographic information, and answering geographic questions) to analyze a geographic problem or issue.

Examples may include but are not limited to: analyzing the natural and human factors that limited the extent of the Roman Empire.
