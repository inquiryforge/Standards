# W3.1.7 - Use a case study to describe how trade integrated cultures and influenced the...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Use a case study to describe how trade integrated cultures and influenced the economy within empires.

Examples may include but are not limited to: Assyrian and Persian Empires, Egypt and Nubia/Kush, Phoenician and Greek networks, early Silk Road, Mesoamerican and Andean Empires.
