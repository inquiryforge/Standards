# G5.1.2 - Explain how different technologies were used in the era being studied.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain how different technologies were used in the era being studied.

Examples may include but are not limited to: irrigation in major river valley civilizations, island creation among the Aztecs, iron technology in Africa, silk and pottery technology in China.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
