# W4.1.1 - Crisis in the Classical World

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Crisis in the Classical World – analyze the environmental, economic, and political crises in the classical world that led to the collapse of classical empires and the consolidation of Byzantium.

Examples may include but are not limited to: the fall of Rome, collapse of the Mayans, demise of the Incan Empire.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
