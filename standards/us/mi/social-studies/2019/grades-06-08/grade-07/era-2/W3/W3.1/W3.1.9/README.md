# W3.1.9 - Describe the significance of legal codes, belief systems, written languages, and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe the significance of legal codes, belief systems, written languages, and communications in the development of large regional empires.

Examples may include but are not limited to: Mesopotamian Empires: cuneiform, Code of Hammurabi; the Ten Commandments, the Tang Code; the Roman Justinian Code; Indian Empires: Sanskrit; Nile River Empires: hieroglyphs; Chinese Empire: character writing, belief system of Confucianism, Daoism, Legalism; American Empires: Incan knot language, Mayan codices.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
