# WHG ERA 2 – EARLY CIVILIZATIONS AND CULTURES AND THE EMERGENCE OF PASTORAL PEOPLES, 4000 TO 1000 BCE AND WESTERN HEMISPHERE 4000 BCE to 1500 CE

[Parent](../README.md)

## Source text

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe and compare defining characteristics of early civilization and pastoral societies, where they emerged, and how they spread. This era includes civilizations in AfroEurasia from 4000 to 1000 BCE as well as cultures in developing the Western Hemisphere from 4000 BCE into Eras 3 and 4 so teachers can compare early civilizations around the globe.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
