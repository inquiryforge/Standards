# W2.1.6 - Describe pastoralism and explain how the climate and geography of Central Asia...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe pastoralism and explain how the climate and geography of Central Asia were linked to the rise of pastoral societies on the steppes.

Examples may include but are not limited to: the steppes of Central Asia, the savannas of East Africa, the tundra of northern Eurasia, or the mountains of Tibet or South America.
