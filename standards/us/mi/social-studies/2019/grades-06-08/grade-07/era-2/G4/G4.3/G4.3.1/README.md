# G4.3.1 - Explain how people in the past have modified the environment and used technology...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain how people in the past have modified the environment and used technology to make places more suitable for humans.

Examples may include but are not limited to: agricultural technologies including irrigation.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
