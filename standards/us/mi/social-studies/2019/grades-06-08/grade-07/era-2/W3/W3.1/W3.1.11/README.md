# W3.1.11 - Explain the role of economics in shaping the development of classical...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain the role of economics in shaping the development of classical civilizations and empires.

Examples may include but are not limited to: trade routes and their significance, and supply and demand for products.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
