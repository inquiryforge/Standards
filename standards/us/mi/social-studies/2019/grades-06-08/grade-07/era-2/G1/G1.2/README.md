# G1.2 - Geographical Inquiry and Analysis

[Parent](../README.md)

## Source text

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Use geographical inquiry and analysis to answer questions about relationships between peoples, cultures, and their environments, and interaction among places and cultures within the era under study.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
