# W4.1.3 - North America to 1500 CE

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
North America to 1500 CE – use a case study to describe the culture and economy of Indigenous Peoples in North America prior to 1500.

Examples may include but are not limited to: Eastern Woodland (Iroquois, Anishinaabek), Southeast (Cherokee, Seminole), Middle America/Mexico (Aztec), Southwest (Navajo, Apache), Northwest (Salish, Muckleshoot), and Great Plains (Lakota, Blackfeet).
