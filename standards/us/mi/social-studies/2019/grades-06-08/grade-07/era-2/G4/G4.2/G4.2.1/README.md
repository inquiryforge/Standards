# G4.2.1 - Identify and describe the advantages, disadvantages, and impacts of different...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify and describe the advantages, disadvantages, and impacts of different technologies used to transport products and ideas in the era being studied.

Examples may include but are not limited to: the development of the wheel; different sail, boat, and navigation technologies; road-building technologies in the Incan and Roman Empires; the use of horses in different cultures.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
