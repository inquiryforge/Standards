# W2.1.4 - Examine early civilizations to describe their common features, including...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Examine early civilizations to describe their common features, including environment, economies, and social institutions.

Examples may include but are not limited to: the Nile, Tigris/Euphrates, and Indus river civilizations in deserts, and Huang He and Mississippi river valley civilizations, and Mesoamerican and Andean civilizations. Topics might include ways of governing, stable food supplies, economic and social structures, use of resources and technology, division of labor, and forms of communication.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
