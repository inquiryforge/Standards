# G4.4.2 - Describe examples of cooperation and conflict in the era being studied.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe examples of cooperation and conflict in the era being studied.

Examples may include but are not limited to: conflict among Greek states and the Persian Empire or the expansion of the Roman Empire and the later invasions into the Roman Empire; examples of cooperation through trade/peaceful co-existence include the development of early exchange routes between Europe and Asia and Africa.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
