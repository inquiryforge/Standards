# G3 - INVESTIGATION AND ANALYSIS

[Parent](../README.md)

## Source text

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Throughout the school year, the students are introduced to topics that address issues that integrate time and place. Included are capstone projects that entail the investigation of historical issues that have significance for the student and are clearly linked to the world outside the classroom. The topics and issues are developed as possible capstone projects within units and at the end of the course.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
