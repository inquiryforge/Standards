# W4.1.2 - Africa to 1500 CE

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Africa to 1500 CE – use a case study to describe how trade integrated cultures and influenced the economy within early African empires.

Examples may include but are not limited to: comparing characteristics of Aksum, Ghana, Mali, or Songhai civilizations; interpreting maps of the Trans-Saharan trade in gold and salt.
