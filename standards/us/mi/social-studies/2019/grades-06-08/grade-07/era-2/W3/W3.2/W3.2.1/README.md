# W3.2.1 - Identify and describe the core beliefs of major world religions and belief...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify and describe the core beliefs of major world religions and belief systems, including Hinduism, Judaism, Buddhism, Christianity, Confucianism, Sikhism and Islam.

Examples may include, but are not limited to: comparing major figures, sacred texts, and basic beliefs (ethnic vs. universalizing; monotheistic vs. polytheistic) among religions; case studies of continuity of local indigenous belief systems or animistic religions; comparisons with religious traditions that developed after 1500 CE such as Protestantism.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
