# P1.3 - Express social science ideas clearly in written, spoken, and graphic forms,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Express social science ideas clearly in written, spoken, and graphic forms, including tables, line graphs, bar graphs, pie charts, maps, and images.
