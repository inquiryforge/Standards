# U4.3.2 - Describe the formation and development of the abolitionist movement by considering...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe the formation and development of the abolitionist movement by considering the roles of key abolitionist leaders and the response of southerners and northerners to the abolitionist movement.

Examples may include but are not limited to: John Brown and the armed resistance, Harriet Tubman, the Underground Railroad, Sojourner Truth, Maria Stewart, William Lloyd Garrison, and Frederick Douglass.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
