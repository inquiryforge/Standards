# U4.1.1 - Washington’s Farewell

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Washington’s Farewell – use President George Washington’s farewell address to analyze Washington’s perspective on the most significant challenges the new nation faced.

Examples may include but are not limited to: deciding if and when to get involved in foreign conflicts, the risk of political factions, establishing the limits of executive power.
