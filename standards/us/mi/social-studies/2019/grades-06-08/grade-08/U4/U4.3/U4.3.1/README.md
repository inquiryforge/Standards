# U4.3.1 - Explain the origins of the American education system.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain the origins of the American education system.

Examples may include but are not limited to: the contributions of Benjamin Franklin, Benjamin Rush, Noah Webster, and Horace Mann.
