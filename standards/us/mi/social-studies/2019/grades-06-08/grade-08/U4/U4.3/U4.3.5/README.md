# U4.3.5 - Investigate the role of religion in shaping antebellum reform movements.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Investigate the role of religion in shaping antebellum reform movements.

Examples may include but are not limited to: differences in beliefs by different denominations of Christianity.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
