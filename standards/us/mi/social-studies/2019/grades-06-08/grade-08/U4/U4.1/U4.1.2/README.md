# U4.1.2 - Establishing America’s Place in the World

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Establishing America’s Place in the World – assess the changes in America’s relationships with other nations by analyzing the origins, intents, and purposes of treaties.

Examples may include but are not limited to: The Jay Treaty (1795), French Revolution, Pinckney’s Treaty (1795), Louisiana Purchase, War of 1812, and the Monroe Doctrine.
