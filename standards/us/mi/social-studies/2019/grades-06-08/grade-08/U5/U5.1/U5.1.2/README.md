# U5.1.2 - Describe the impact of the Northwest Ordinance on the expansion of slavery.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe the impact of the Northwest Ordinance on the expansion of slavery.

Examples may include but are not limited to: the establishment of free states, including Michigan, as a result of the Northwest Ordinance.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
