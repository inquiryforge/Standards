# U5.1.3 - Describe the competing views of John C. Calhoun, Daniel Webster, and Henry Clay on...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe the competing views of John C. Calhoun, Daniel Webster, and Henry Clay on the nature of the union among the states.

Examples may include but are not limited to: sectionalism, nationalism, federalism, state rights.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
