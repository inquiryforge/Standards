# U5 - USHG ERA 5 – CIVIL WAR AND RECONSTRUCTION (1850-1877)

[Parent](../README.md)

## Source text

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Individually and collaboratively, students will engage in planned inquiries to understand the causes, course, and character of the Civil War and its effects on people, as well as how various Reconstruction plans succeeded or failed.
