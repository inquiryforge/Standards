# U5.1.5 - Describe the resistance of enslaved persons and effects of their actions before...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe the resistance of enslaved persons and effects of their actions before and during the Civil War.

Examples may include but are not limited to: Nat Turner, Harriet Tubman and the Underground Railroad, Michigan’s role in the Underground Railroad.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
