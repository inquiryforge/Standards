# FOUNDATIONS IN U.S. HISTORY AND GEOGRAPHY ERAS 1-2

[Parent](../README.md)

## Source text

<!-- standard-text:start -->
These foundational expectations are included to help students draw upon their previous study of American history and connect 8th Grade U.S. History with the history studied in 5th grade.

To set the stage for the study of U.S. history that begins with the development of the U.S. Constitution, students should be able to draw upon an understanding of these philosophies and intellectual foundations.
