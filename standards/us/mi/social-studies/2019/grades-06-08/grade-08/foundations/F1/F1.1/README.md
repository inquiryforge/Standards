# F1.1 - Describe the ideas, experiences, and interactions that influenced the colonists’...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe the ideas, experiences, and interactions that influenced the colonists’ decisions to declare independence by analyzing:

- colonial ideas about government.

- experiences with self-government.

Examples may include but are not limited to: limited government, republicanism, protecting individual rights and promoting the common good, representative government, natural rights, House of Burgesses and town meetings, changing interactions with the royal government of Great Britain after the French and Indian War.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
