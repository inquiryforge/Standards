# U3.3.1 - Explain the reasons for the adoption and subsequent failure of the Articles of...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain the reasons for the adoption and subsequent failure of the Articles of Confederation.

Examples may include but are not limited to: why its drafters created a weak central government, challenges the nation faced under the Articles, Shay’s Rebellion, conflicts over western lands.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
