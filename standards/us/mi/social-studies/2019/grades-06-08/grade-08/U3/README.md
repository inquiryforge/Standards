# U3 - USHG ERA 3 – REVOLUTION AND THE NEW NATION

[Parent](../README.md)

## Source text

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Individually and collaboratively, students will engage in planned inquiries to analyze the institutions and practices of government created during the Revolution and how they were revised between 1787 and 1815 to create the foundation of the American political system.
