# C3.6.2 - Compare and contrast various forms of government around the world.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Compare and contrast various forms of government around the world.

Examples may include but are not limited to: democracies, parliamentary systems, dictatorships, oligarchies, and theocracies.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
