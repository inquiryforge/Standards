# C3.6.1 - Define the characteristics of modern nation-states.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Define the characteristics of modern nation-states.

Examples may include but are not limited to: a specific territory, clearly defined boundaries, citizens, collect taxes and provide services, jurisdiction over people who reside there, laws, and government.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
