# C4.3.1 - Explain how governments address national and international issues and form...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain how governments address national and international issues and form policies, and how the policies may not be consistent with those of other nation-states.

Examples may include but are not limited to: climate change, and human and civil rights; within the United States, federal/tribal relations in the United States.
