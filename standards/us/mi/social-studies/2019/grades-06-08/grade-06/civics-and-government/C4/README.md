# C4 - RELATIONSHIP OF UNITED STATES TO OTHER GOVERNMENTS, WORLD ISSUES, AND WORLD GOVERNING ORGANIZATIONS

[Parent](../README.md)

## Source text

<!-- standard-text:start -->
