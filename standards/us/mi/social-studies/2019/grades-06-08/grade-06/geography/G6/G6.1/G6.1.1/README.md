# G6.1.1 - Identify global issues.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify global issues.

Examples may include but are not limited to: natural disasters, immigration, food production, food distribution, the impact of climate change, population growth, resource use and depletion, meeting the needs of refugees, migration, poverty, economic development, conflict, and terrorism.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
