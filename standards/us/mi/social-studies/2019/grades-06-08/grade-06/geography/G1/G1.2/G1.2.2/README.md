# G1.2.2 - Explain why maps of the same place may vary, including the perspectives and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain why maps of the same place may vary, including the perspectives and purposes of the cartographers.

Examples may include but are not limited to: different countries may label disputed territories differently, remote sensing images provide information not visible to humans.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
