# G4.4.2 - Evaluate examples of cooperation and conflict within the region under study from...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Evaluate examples of cooperation and conflict within the region under study from different perspectives.

Examples may include but are not limited to: cooperation between the United States and Canada to protect the fresh water of the Great Lakes, cooperation efforts to stop the spread of diseases among populations, or conflict over control of islands in the South China Sea.
