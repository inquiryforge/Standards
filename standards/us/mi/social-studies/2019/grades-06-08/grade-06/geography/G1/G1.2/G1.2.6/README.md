# G1.2.6 - Create or interpret a map of the population distribution of a region and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Create or interpret a map of the population distribution of a region and generalize about the factors influencing the distribution of the population.

Examples may include but are not limited to: how natural characteristics are associated with sparse population densities, how different combinations of natural and human factors lead to different densities, and why major cities are located where they are.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
