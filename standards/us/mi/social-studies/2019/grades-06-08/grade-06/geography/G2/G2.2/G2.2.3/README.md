# G2.2.3 - Explain how culture and experience influence people’s perceptions of places and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain how culture and experience influence people’s perceptions of places and regions.

Examples may include but are not limited to: how an immigrant and a resident might view a community, how a tourist might see a culture differently than someone who was born and lives there, and how international travel might change a person’s perspective.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
