# G6.1.2 - Investigate a contemporary global issue by applying the skills of geographic inquiry.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Investigate a contemporary global issue by applying the skills of geographic inquiry.

Examples may include but are not limited to: asking geographic questions; acquiring, organizing, and analyzing geographic information; answering geographic questions when practical; using inquiry methods to acquire content knowledge and appropriate data about the issue; identifying the causes and consequences and analyzing the impact, both positive and negative.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
