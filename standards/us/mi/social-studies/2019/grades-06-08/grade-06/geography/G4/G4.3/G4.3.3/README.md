# G4.3.3 - Explain the patterns, causes, and consequences of major human migrations.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain the patterns, causes, and consequences of major human migrations.

Examples may include but are not limited to: refugee migrations, economic migrations, seasonal migration, and migrations from rural to urban.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
