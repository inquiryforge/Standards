# G5.2.2 - Analyze how combinations of human decisions and natural forces can lead to (or...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Analyze how combinations of human decisions and natural forces can lead to (or help people avoid) a natural disaster.

Examples may include but are not limited to: how building in flood plains increases the likelihood of a natural disaster, and how the federal Soil Conservation Service works to prevent a natural disaster, such as the Dust Bowl.
