# G1.2.4 - Use images as the basis for answering geographic questions about the human and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Use images as the basis for answering geographic questions about the human and physical characteristics of places and major world regions.

Examples may include but are not limited to: pictures, aerial photos, and remote sensing images.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
