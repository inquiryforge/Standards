# G1.2.5 - Locate and use information from GIS and satellite remote sensing to answer...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Locate and use information from GIS and satellite remote sensing to answer geographic questions.

Examples may include but are not limited to: Google Earth and ArcGIS on-line have multiple teacher applications. Clickable PDFs provide overlay strategies for students without technology skills.
