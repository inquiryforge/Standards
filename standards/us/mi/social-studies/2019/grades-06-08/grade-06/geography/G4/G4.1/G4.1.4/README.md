# G4.1.4 - Explain how culture influences the daily lives of people.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain how culture influences the daily lives of people.

Examples may include but are not limited to: how people make a living, raise families, educate children and practice their religion in different cultures and communities.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
