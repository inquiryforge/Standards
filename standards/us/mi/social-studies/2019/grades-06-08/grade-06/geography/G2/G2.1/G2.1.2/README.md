# G2.1.2 - Locate and describe the basic patterns and processes of plate tectonics.

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Locate and describe the basic patterns and processes of plate tectonics.

Examples may include but are not limited to: the location of continental plates and the Ring of Fire. Processes include plate movement, uplift, earthquakes, and volcanism.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
