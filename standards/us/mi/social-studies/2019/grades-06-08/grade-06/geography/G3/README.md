# G3 - PHYSICAL SYSTEMS

[Parent](../README.md)

## Source text

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe the physical processes that shape the Earth’s surface that, along with plants and animals, are the basis for both sustaining and modifying ecosystems. Identify and analyze the patterns and characteristics of the major ecosystems on Earth.
