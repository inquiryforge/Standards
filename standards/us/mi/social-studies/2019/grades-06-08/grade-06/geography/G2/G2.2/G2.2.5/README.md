# G2.2.5 - Generalize about how human and natural factors have influenced how people make a...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Generalize about how human and natural factors have influenced how people make a living and perform other activities in a place.

Examples may include but are not limited to: how physical features, including mountains, rivers, coasts, deserts, and natural resources, as well as human factors such as political boundaries and accessibility, can affect community size and location. Additional examples might include how groups of people with different levels of economic or political power might choose or be restricted to different locations.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
