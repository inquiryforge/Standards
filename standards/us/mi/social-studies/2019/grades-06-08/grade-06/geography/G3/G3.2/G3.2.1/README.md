# G3.2.1 - Locate major ecosystems and explain how and why they are similar or different as a...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Locate major ecosystems and explain how and why they are similar or different as a consequence of latitude, elevation, land-forms, location, and human activity.

Examples may include but are not limited to: deciduous forest versus prairies in the United States, tropical rain forest versus savanna and desert in Africa, and tundra versus coniferous forests in Canada or Russia.
