# G4.3.2 - Describe patterns of settlement and explain why people settle where they do and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe patterns of settlement and explain why people settle where they do and how people make their livings.

Examples may include but are not limited to: coastal and river towns in the past and present, the location of mega-cities, and how people make their livings in different locations. Examples also include forced settlement and/or restrictions on resettlement.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
