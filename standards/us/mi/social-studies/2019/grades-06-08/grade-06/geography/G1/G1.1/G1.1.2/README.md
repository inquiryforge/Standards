# G1.1.2 - Draw a sketch map, or add information to an outline map, of the world or a world...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Draw a sketch map, or add information to an outline map, of the world or a world region.

Examples may include but are not limited to: locate the following on a world map: the United States, North and South America, Africa, Europe, and Asia; continents; oceans; and latitude lines — equator, Prime Meridian, tropics of Cancer and Capricorn, Arctic and Antarctic circles using Geographic Information Systems (GIS), drawing, or web-based programs.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
