# G2 - PLACES AND REGIONS

[Parent](../README.md)

## Source text

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe the cultural groups and diversities among people who are rooted in particular places and in human constructs called regions. Analyze the physical and human characteristics of places and regions.
