# E3.3.3 - Explain how communication innovations have affected economic interactions and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain how communication innovations have affected economic interactions and where and how people work.

Examples may include but are not limited to: Internet-based home offices, international work teams, international companies, online shopping.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
