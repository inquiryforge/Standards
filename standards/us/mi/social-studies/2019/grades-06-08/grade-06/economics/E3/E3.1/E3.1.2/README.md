# E3.1.2 - Compare and contrast the economic and ecological costs and benefits of different...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Compare and contrast the economic and ecological costs and benefits of different kinds of energy production.

Examples may include but are not limited to: oil, coal, natural gas, nuclear, biomass, solar, hydroelectric, geothermal, wind, and the impact of each.
