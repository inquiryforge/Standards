# E3.3.2 - Diagram or map the flow of materials, labor, and capital used to produce a...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Diagram or map the flow of materials, labor, and capital used to produce a consumer product.

Examples may include but are not limited to: global supply chain, computer production, automobile production.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
