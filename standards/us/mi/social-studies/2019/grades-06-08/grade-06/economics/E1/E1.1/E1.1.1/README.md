# E1.1.1 - Explain how incentives and disincentives in the market economy can change the...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain how incentives and disincentives in the market economy can change the decision-making process.

Examples may include but are not limited to: acquiring money, profit, and goods; wanting to avoid loss of position in society; job placement; taxes on cigarettes to discourage smoking; raising prices to increase profit.
