# 2.2.M.F.d - Compare the significance of the products associated with an important: • civil or...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Compare the significance of the products associated with an important: • civil or religious holiday or celebration AND • regional holiday or celebration AND • personal or family holiday or celebration across multiple communities or cultures in which the target language is spoken
<!-- standard-text:end -->
