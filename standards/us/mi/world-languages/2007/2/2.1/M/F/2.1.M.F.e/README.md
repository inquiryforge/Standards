# 2.1.M.F.e - Compare the practices and significance of an important: • civil or religious holiday...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Compare the practices and significance of an important: • civil or religious holiday or celebration AND • regional holiday or celebration AND • personal or family holiday or celebration across multiple communities or cultures in which the target language is spoken
<!-- standard-text:end -->
