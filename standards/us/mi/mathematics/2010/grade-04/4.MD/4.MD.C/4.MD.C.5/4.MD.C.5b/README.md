# 4.MD.C.5b - An angle that turns through n one-degree angles is said to have an angle measure of...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
An angle that turns through n one-degree angles is said to have an angle measure of n degrees. ^(4)Students who can generate equivalent fractions can develop strategies for adding fractions with unlike denominators in general. But addition and subtraction with un-like denominators in general is not a requirement at this grade.
