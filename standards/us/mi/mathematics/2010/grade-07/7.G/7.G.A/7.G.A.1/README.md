# 7.G.A.1 - Solve problems involving scale drawings of geometric figures, including computing...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Solve problems involving scale drawings of geometric figures, including computing actual lengths and areas from a scale drawing and reproducing a scale drawing at a different scale. ^(1)Computations with rational numbers extend the rules for manipulating fractions to complex fractions.
