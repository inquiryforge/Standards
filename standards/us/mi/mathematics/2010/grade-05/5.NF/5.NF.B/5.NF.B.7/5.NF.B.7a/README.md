# 5.NF.B.7a - Interpret division of a unit fraction by a non-zero whole number, ^(1)Students able...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Interpret division of a unit fraction by a non-zero whole number, ^(1)Students able to multiply fractions in general can develop strategies to divide frac-tions in general, by reasoning about the relationship between multiplication and division. But division of a fraction by a fraction is not a requirement at this grade. and compute such quotients. For example, create a story context for (1/3) ÷ 4, and use a visual fraction model to show the quotient. Use the relationship between multiplication and division to explain that (1/3) ÷ 4 = 1/12 because (1/12) × 4 = 1/3.
