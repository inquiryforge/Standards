# N-RN.A.1 - Explain how the definition of the meaning of rational exponents follows from...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain how the definition of the meaning of rational exponents follows from extending the properties of integer exponents to those values, allowing for a notation for radicals in terms of rational exponents. For example, we defifine 5^(1/3) to be the cube root of 5 because we want (5^(1/3))^(3) = 5^((1/3)3) to hold, so (5^(1/3))^(3) must equal 5.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
