# N-CN.A.1 - Know there is a complex number i such that i^(2) = –1, and every complex number has...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Know there is a complex number i such that i^(2) = –1, and every complex number has the form a + bi with a and b real.
