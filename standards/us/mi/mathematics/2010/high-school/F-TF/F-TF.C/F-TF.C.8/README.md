# F-TF.C.8 - Prove the Pythagorean identity sin^(2)(θ) + cos^(2)(θ) = 1 and use it to fifind...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Prove the Pythagorean identity sin^(2)(θ) + cos^(2)(θ) = 1 and use it to fifind sin(θ), cos(θ), or tan(θ) given sin(θ), cos(θ), or tan(θ) and the quadrant of the angle.
