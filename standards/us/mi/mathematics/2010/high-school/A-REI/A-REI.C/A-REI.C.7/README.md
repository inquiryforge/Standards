# A-REI.C.7 - Solve a simple system consisting of a linear equation and a quadratic equation in...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Solve a simple system consisting of a linear equation and a quadratic equation in two variables algebraically and graphically. For example, fifind the points of intersection between the line y = –3x and the circle x^(2) + y^(2) = 3.
