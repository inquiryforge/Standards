# 2.G.A.3 - Partition circles and rectangles into two, three, or four equal shares, describe the...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Partition circles and rectangles into two, three, or four equal shares, describe the shares using the words halves, thirds, half of, a third of, etc., and describe the whole as two halves, three thirds, four fourths. Recognize that equal shares of identical wholes need not have the same shape. ^(4)See Glossary, Table 1. ^(5)Sizes are compared directly or visually, not compared by measuring.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
