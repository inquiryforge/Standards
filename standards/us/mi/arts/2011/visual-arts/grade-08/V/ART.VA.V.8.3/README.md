# ART.VA.V.8.3 - Effectively analyze and compare the characteristics of work in two or more art forms...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Effectively analyze and compare the characteristics of work in two or more art forms that are dissimilar in subject matter, historical periods, or cultural context.
