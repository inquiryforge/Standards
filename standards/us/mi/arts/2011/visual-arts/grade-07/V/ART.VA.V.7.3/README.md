# ART.VA.V.7.3 - Analyze and compare the characteristics of work in two or more art forms that are...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Analyze and compare the characteristics of work in two or more art forms that are dissimilar in subject matter, historical periods, or cultural context at an emerging level.
