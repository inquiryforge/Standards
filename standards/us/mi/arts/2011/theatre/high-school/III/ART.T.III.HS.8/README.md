# ART.T.III.HS.8 - Analyze and critique the whole and the parts of dramatic performances, taking into...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Analyze and critique the whole and the parts of dramatic performances, taking into account the context, and constructively suggest alternative artistic choices including visual and aural components influenced by the use of technology. (21st Century Skills: I.3, I.4, I.6, II.3, II.4, II.5)
