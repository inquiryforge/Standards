# ART.T.III.HS.7 - Identify and research cultural, historical, and symbolic clues in dramatic texts,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify and research cultural, historical, and symbolic clues in dramatic texts, and evaluate the validity and practicality of the information to assist in making artistic choices for informal and formal productions.
