# ART.M.II.8.3 - Improvise short melodies unaccompanied and over given rhythmic accompaniments, each...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Improvise short melodies unaccompanied and over given rhythmic accompaniments, each in a consistent style, meter, and tonality.
