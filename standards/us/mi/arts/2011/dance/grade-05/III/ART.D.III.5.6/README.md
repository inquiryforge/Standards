# ART.D.III.5.6 - Observe two dances and discuss how they are similar and different in terms of one of...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Observe two dances and discuss how they are similar and different in terms of one of the elements of dance by observing body shapes, levels, and pathways.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
