# ART.D.IV.6.2 - Introduce, research, and explore folk, social, or theatrical dance forms learned...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Introduce, research, and explore folk, social, or theatrical dance forms learned from resources in their own community, of different cultures, time periods, or from a broad spectrum of twentieth-century America. Describe the cultural/historical context of that dance, effectively sharing its context with peers.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
