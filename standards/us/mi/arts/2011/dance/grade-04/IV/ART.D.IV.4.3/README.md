# ART.D.IV.4.3 - Ask parents or some other adult about dances done in their community when they were...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Ask parents or some other adult about dances done in their community when they were younger. Ask if the person will teach you the dance and tell you about it. Share your findings with your classmates.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
