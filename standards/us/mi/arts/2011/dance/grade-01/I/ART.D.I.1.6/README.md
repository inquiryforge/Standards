# ART.D.I.1.6 - Identify and name parts of the body, as they are touched: head, neck, shoulders,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify and name parts of the body, as they are touched: head, neck, shoulders, hips, arms, legs, and knees. RT.D.I.1.7 Demonstrate movement by the ability to walk with a partner.
