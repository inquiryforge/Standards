# ART.D.I.HS.1 - Demonstrate appropriate skeletal alignment, body-part articulation, strength,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate appropriate skeletal alignment, body-part articulation, strength, flexibility, agility, and coordination in locomotor and non-locomotor/axial movements. ART.D.1.HS.2 Identify and demonstrate longer and more complex steps and patterns from two different dance styles/traditions. ART.D.1.HS.3 Demonstrate rhythmic acuity. ART.D.1.HS.4 Demonstrate projection while performing dance skills. ART.D.1.HS.5 Demonstrate the ability to remember and perform extended movement sequences.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
