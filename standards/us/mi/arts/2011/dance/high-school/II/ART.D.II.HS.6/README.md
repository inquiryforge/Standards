# ART.D.II.HS.6 - Create a dance and revise it over time using multi-media equipment, such as slides,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Create a dance and revise it over time using multi-media equipment, such as slides, camera, video, and computers to articulate the reasons for artistic decisions and what was lost and gained by those decisions.
