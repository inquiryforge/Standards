# B6.S031 - Establish a pitch attitude for VY and accelerate to VY +10/-5 knots after clearing...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Establish a pitch attitude for VY and accelerate to VY +10/-5 knots after clearing the obstacle or at 50 feet AGL if simulating an obstacle.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
