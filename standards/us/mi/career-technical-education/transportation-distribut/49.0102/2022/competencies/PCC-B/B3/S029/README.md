# B3.S029 - Maintain elevator as recommended by manufacturer during rollout and exit the “soft”...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Maintain elevator as recommended by manufacturer during rollout and exit the “soft” area at a speed that would preclude sinking into the surface.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
