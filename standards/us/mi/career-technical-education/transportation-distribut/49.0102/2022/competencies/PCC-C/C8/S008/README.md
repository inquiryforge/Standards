# C8.S008 - Use flight controls in the proper combination as recommended by the manufacturer, or...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Use flight controls in the proper combination as recommended by the manufacturer, or as required to maintain best performance, and trim as required.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
