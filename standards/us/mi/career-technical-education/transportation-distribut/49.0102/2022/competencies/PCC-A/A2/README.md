# A2 - Obtain medical certificates and satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Obtain medical certificates and satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with personal health, flight physiology, aeromedical, and human factors as they relate to safety of flight.
