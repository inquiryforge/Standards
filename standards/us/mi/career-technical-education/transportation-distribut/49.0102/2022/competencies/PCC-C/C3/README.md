# C3 - Fly using altitude instruments during turns to headings and recover from unusual...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Fly using altitude instruments during turns to headings and recover from unusual altitudes flying during constant airspeed climbs and descents solely by reference to instruments.
