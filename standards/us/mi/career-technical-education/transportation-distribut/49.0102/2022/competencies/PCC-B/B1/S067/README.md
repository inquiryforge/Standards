# B1.S067 - Maintain manufacturer’s published approach airspeed or in its absence not more than...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Maintain manufacturer’s published approach airspeed or in its absence not more than 1.3 VSO, +10/-5 knots with gust factor applied.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
