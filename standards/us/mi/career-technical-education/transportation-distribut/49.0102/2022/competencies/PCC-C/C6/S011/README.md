# C6.S011 - Turn to headings, maintain altitude ±200 feet, maintain a standard rate turn, roll...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Turn to headings, maintain altitude ±200 feet, maintain a standard rate turn, roll out on the assigned heading ±10°, and maintain airspeed ±10 knots.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
