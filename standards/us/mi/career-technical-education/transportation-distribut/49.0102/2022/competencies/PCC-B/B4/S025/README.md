# B4.S025 - Rotate and lift off at the recommended airspeed and accelerate to the recommended...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Rotate and lift off at the recommended airspeed and accelerate to the recommended obstacle clearance airspeed or VX, +10/-5 knots.
