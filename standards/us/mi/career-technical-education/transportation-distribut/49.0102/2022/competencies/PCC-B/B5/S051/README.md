# B5.S051 - One solo cross country flight of 150 nautical miles total distance, with full-stop...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
One solo cross country flight of 150 nautical miles total distance, with full-stop landings at three points and one segment of the flight consisting of a straight-line distance of more than 50 nautical miles between take off and landing locations; and Three takeoffs and landings to a full stop (with each landing involving a flight in the traffic pattern) at an airport with an operating control tower.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
