# X.AP.10.c - Disposition of hazardous materials is dependent upon the commodity that is being...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Disposition of hazardous materials is dependent upon the commodity that is being discarded. Disposal must be in accordance with federal, state and local hazardous material disposal procedures.
