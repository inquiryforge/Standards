# X.AP.4.c - Hardware Removal, Control and Replacement—If hardware is removed during maintenance,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Hardware Removal, Control and Replacement—If hardware is removed during maintenance, alteration or other task the removed hardware must be controlled and accounted for. Procedures used to control and account for removed hardware may differ from one organization to another, but established procedures must be followed. Failure to follow established procedures is often a major contributor in the creation of foreign objects.
