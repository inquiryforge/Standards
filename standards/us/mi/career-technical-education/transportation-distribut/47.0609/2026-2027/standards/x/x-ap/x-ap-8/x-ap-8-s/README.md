# X.AP.8.s - Transportation—Follow transportation procedures to prevent damage. Transporting FOD...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Transportation—Follow transportation procedures to prevent damage. Transporting FOD sensitive products can be a cause of Foreign Object Damage. This consideration includes the responsibility of transportation vehicles to be free of loose debris while transporting FOD sensitive products.
