# X.AP.11.b - Environment—Elements of weather are considered foreign objects. Snow, rain, hail,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Environment—Elements of weather are considered foreign objects. Snow, rain, hail, wind, and ice are foreign to the aircraft, product, or assembly and can cause damage.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
