# X.AP.1.a - FO (Foreign Object)—Sometimes referred to as Foreign Object Debris (FOD) – Any alien...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
FO (Foreign Object)—Sometimes referred to as Foreign Object Debris (FOD) – Any alien substance or article that invades any component of the aircraft and which causes or has potential to cause damage to aircraft, persons, equipment, or otherwise diminish safety.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
