# X.AP.6.d - FOD designated areas whether found in manufacturing operations, repair station or...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
FOD designated areas whether found in manufacturing operations, repair station or general repair facilities, are selected and defined by the individual aerospace operator in accordance with their particular operational requirements. FOD designated areas are typically identified as: FOD Awareness, FOD Sensitive, FOD Critical, FOD Restricted and FOD Controlled. Entry into these areas is limited in accordance with the organization’s established FOD/FOE Program Procedures. The procedures may include training and/or certification(s) coupled to appropriate authorization.
