# X.AP.8.q - Opening and Closing Containers—Caution must be taken when opening and closing...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Opening and Closing Containers—Caution must be taken when opening and closing containers. Nailing, strapping or installing screws can cause damage to hardware or provide additional internal FOD in the container.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
