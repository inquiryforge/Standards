# X.AP.3.c - Tether—A lanyard of sufficient length and strength (wire, rope, cable, etc.) that is...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Tether—A lanyard of sufficient length and strength (wire, rope, cable, etc.) that is secured to the technician (user), structural workstand or other suitable location at one end (as appropriate) and the tool or equipment at the other end. The tether should be minimum length to preclude damage from tethered tool “free swing.” Tethering prevents dropping tools or equipment into or onto aircraft or other aerospace products. The tether itself can become FOD if it (including tether related hardware) is not regularly examined for damage and wear.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
