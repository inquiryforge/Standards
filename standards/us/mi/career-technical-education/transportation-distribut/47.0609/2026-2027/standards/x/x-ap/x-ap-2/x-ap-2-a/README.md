# X.AP.2.a - Clean As You Go Programs utilize practices to prevent FOD from migrating into...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Clean As You Go Programs utilize practices to prevent FOD from migrating into aviation products. Clean As You Go Programs provide an ongoing process of removing work debris as it accumulates and keeps the work area clean while tasks are performed on products, vehicles, or assemblies in a FOD designated area.
