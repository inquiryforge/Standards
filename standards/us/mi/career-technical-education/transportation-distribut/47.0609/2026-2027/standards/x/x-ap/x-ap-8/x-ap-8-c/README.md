# X.AP.8.c - Materials and accessories used in the packaging, handling, shipping and storage that...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Materials and accessories used in the packaging, handling, shipping and storage that have intimate contact with the part or assembly should be clean and free of contamination.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
