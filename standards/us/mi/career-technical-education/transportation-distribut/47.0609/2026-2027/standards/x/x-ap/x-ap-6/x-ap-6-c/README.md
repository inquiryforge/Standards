# X.AP.6.c - Most manufacturing companies identify FOD designated areas based on a risk...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Most manufacturing companies identify FOD designated areas based on a risk assessment of the potential for FO to migrate into the final product. Requirements for entry into and working within these areas increase as the final product progresses through the factory to customer delivery.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
