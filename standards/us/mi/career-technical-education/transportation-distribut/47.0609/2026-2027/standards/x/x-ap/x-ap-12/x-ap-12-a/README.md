# X.AP.12.a - Costs—The aviation and aerospace industry is losing approximately 4 billion dollars...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Costs—The aviation and aerospace industry is losing approximately 4 billion dollars annually to FOD. Insurance expenses (cost) to companies that have incidents are increasing. In the manufacturing sector manufacturers may increase contract cost, which may lead to the loss of current or future contracts. In other aerospace operations (air carrier, repair organization or general aviation) the cost can easily cause the failure of the business.
