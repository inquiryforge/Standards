# X.AP.4.d - Hardware Storage— During the manufacturer, maintenance, repair or alteration of an...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Hardware Storage— During the manufacturer, maintenance, repair or alteration of an aerospace product, assembly or vehicle it is critical to have hardware contained in a manner that does not promote migration to the product as FOD. As hardware storage moves closer to FOD designated areas, greater preventive and control features should be implemented.
