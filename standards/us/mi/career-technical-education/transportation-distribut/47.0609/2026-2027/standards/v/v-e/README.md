# V.E - Demonstrate understanding of important trends influencing the TDL system by...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate understanding of important trends influencing the TDL system by assessing and summarizing the impact of various economic, social and technological changes on a TDL organization and its role in the TDL industry.
