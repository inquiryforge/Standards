# X.AP.6.b - Designated Areas—A FOD designated area is any area where flight hardware is in place...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Designated Areas—A FOD designated area is any area where flight hardware is in place and exposure to foreign objects would potentially cause a system or product failure due to deterioration, malfunction or damage.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
