# X.AP.1.e - FOD Designated Area— (Also referred to as FOD Critical (NAS 412) or FOD...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
FOD Designated Area— (Also referred to as FOD Critical (NAS 412) or FOD Sensitive)—Any area where flight hardware is in place and exposure to foreign objects would potentially cause a system or product failure due to deterioration, malfunction or damage.
