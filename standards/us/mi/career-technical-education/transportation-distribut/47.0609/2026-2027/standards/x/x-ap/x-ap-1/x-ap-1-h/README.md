# X.AP.1.h - Critical FO— Any material that is inadvertently left inside an assembly after close...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Critical FO— Any material that is inadvertently left inside an assembly after close out or test and any foreign objects in areas from which migration is possible, (e.g., through tooling holes, bend relief cutouts, drain holes, intakes, etc.) which are probable to cause system or component malfunction or deterioration should the product be put into use.
