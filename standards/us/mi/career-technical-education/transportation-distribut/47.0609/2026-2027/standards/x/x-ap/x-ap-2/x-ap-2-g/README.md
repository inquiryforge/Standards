# X.AP.2.g - Permanent Tooling—At completion of any major or minor permanent tool maintenance or...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Permanent Tooling—At completion of any major or minor permanent tool maintenance or upgrade (may include facilities) FOD designated areas must be completely swept for all foreign objects, ensuring that all foreign objects are removed prior to area activation.
