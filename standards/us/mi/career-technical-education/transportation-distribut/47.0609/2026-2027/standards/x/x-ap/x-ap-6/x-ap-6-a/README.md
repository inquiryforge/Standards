# X.AP.6.a - General Entry—When physical entry is required into flight hardware (e.g., crew...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
General Entry—When physical entry is required into flight hardware (e.g., crew compartment, engine intake, exhaust, fuel tank areas, etc.) personnel should remove all loose objects, badges, jewelry, etc., from clothing. Pocketless or closed zippered pocket coveralls should be worn to preclude foreign objects dropping from pockets.
