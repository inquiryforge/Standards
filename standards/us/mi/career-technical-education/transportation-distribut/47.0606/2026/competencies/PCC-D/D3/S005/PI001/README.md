# D3.S005.PI001 - Remove and inspect the connecting rod and piston, remove and inspect the crankshaft,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Remove and inspect the connecting rod and piston, remove and inspect the crankshaft, remove and replace needle bearings, remove and replace main ball bearings, and inspect, measure, service, or replace all components.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
