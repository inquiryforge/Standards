# A1.S008.PI001 - Select and use proper personal safety equipment for surface preparation, spray gun...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Select and use proper personal safety equipment for surface preparation, spray gun and related equipment operation, paint mixing, matching and application, paint defects, and detailing (glove, suits, hoods, eye and ear protection, etc.); take necessary precautions with hazardous operations and materials according to federal, state, and local regulations.
