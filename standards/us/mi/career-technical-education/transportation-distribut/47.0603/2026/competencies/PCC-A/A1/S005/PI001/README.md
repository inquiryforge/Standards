# A1.S005.PI001 - Demonstrate knowledge of steps needed to assess possible hazards prior to servicing...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate knowledge of steps needed to assess possible hazards prior to servicing high voltage/electric vehicle, including awareness of automatic systems that may operate while the key switch/ignition is off.
