# F1.S004.PI001 - Demonstrate operation and proper use of digital multimeters and other test equipment...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate operation and proper use of digital multimeters and other test equipment when measuring source voltage, voltage drop (including grounds), current flow, continuity, and resistance.
