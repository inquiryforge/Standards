# C1.S012.PI001 - Inspect and lubricate camshafts (S-cams), tubes, rollers, bushings, seals, spacers,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Inspect and lubricate camshafts (S-cams), tubes, rollers, bushings, seals, spacers, retainers, brake spiders, shields, anchor pins, and springs; determine needed action.
