# F1.S034.PI001 - Demonstrate knowledge of the sensor/sending units, gauges, switches, relays,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate knowledge of the sensor/sending units, gauges, switches, relays, bulbs/LEDs, wires, terminals, connectors, sockets, printed circuits, and control components/modules of the instrument cluster, driver information system, and warning systems.
