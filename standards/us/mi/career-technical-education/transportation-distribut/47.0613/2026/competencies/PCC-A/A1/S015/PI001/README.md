# A1.S015.PI001 - Demonstrate knowledge of hazards related to high voltage system/electric vehicles,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate knowledge of hazards related to high voltage system/electric vehicles, including electrocution, fire, explosion, arc flash, gases and fumes, hazardous chemicals, and EMF, and how to properly respond to emergency situations.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
