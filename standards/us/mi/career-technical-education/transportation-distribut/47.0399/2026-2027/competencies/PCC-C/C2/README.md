# C2 - Demonstrate proper setup, operation, and troubleshooting of 12/24 volt systems by...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate proper setup, operation, and troubleshooting of 12/24 volt systems by performing electrical diagnostic tests, isolating faults, and replacing components according to manufacturer specifications.
