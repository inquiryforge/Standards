# II.C.1 - Use Spanish of another language other than English to speak with, write to, and read...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Use Spanish of another language other than English to speak with, write to, and read materials received from persons whose first language is Spanish or another foreign language.
