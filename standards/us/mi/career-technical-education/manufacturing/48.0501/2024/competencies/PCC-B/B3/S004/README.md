# B3.S004 - Describe and use major components and functions of the machine tools, power tools,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe and use major components and functions of the machine tools, power tools, and all major hand tools, measuring tools, tools and fixtures, work materials and provide the rationale for the speeds and feeds selected.
