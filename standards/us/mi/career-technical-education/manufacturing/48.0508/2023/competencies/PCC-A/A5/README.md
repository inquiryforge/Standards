# A5 - Perform safety inspection of thermal cutting and welding equipment/accessories,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Perform safety inspection of thermal cutting and welding equipment/accessories, complete general preventive maintenance, and make minor external repairs when needed.
