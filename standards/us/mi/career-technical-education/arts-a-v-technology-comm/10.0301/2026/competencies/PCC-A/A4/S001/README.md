# A4.S001 - Apply legal and ethical considerations for using third-party content, such as...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Apply legal and ethical considerations for using third-party content, such as copyright, permissions, and licensing, e.g., creative commons, public domain, intellectual property, derivative work, commercial use, attribution, work for hire, fair use, fair dealing, and stock images.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
