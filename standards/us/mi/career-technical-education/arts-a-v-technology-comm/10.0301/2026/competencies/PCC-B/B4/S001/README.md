# B4.S001 - Explain the applications of wide-format printing in the industry and compare various...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain the applications of wide-format printing in the industry and compare various types of wide-format printers (inkjet, solvent, UV, dye-sublimation) with respect to ink types, print quality, substrate compatibility, and durability of output.
