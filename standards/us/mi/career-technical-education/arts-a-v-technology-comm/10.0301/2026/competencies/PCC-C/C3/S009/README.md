# C3.S009 - Collect needed information and complete necessary steps to complete a project from...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Collect needed information and complete necessary steps to complete a project from design to delivery by actively interacting with a client to include: a. Project Initiation (determine the client’s needs) b. Research (explore information provided at the initial client meeting) c. Strategy (analyze the research gathered and decide on design and functionality criteria) d. Development (mind mapping, storyboarding, free writing, layout creation) e. Presentation (review with client; make changes) f. Production/launch (implement finished piece across all deliverables) g. Completion (turn over completed project to client)
<!-- standard-text:end -->
