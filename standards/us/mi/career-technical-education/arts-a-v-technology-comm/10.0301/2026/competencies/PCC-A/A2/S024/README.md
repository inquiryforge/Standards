# A2.S024 - Demonstrate rotating, flipping, modifying individual layers, objects selections,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate rotating, flipping, modifying individual layers, objects selections, groups, and graphical elements, e.g., transforming, warping, distorting, and skewing.
