# A5.S003 - Demonstrate safety habits to protect vision, such as wearing safety glasses when...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate safety habits to protect vision, such as wearing safety glasses when photographing areas that require them and by minimizing time spent focusing on computer screens.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
