# B1.S003 - Examine the history of typography, including its origins, the development of...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Examine the history of typography, including its origins, the development of typefaces, and how technological advances, e.g., the invention of movable type and digital fonts, have influenced design.
