# C3.S011 - Develop a high-quality professional portfolio and resume that effectively showcase...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Develop a high-quality professional portfolio and resume that effectively showcase your design and communication skills, experience, and achievements, tailored to attract potential clients, employers, or collaborators in the graphic design and visual communications industry.
