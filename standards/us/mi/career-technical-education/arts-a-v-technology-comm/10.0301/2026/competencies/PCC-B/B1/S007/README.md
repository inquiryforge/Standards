# B1.S007 - Apply the regulatory roles of the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) in advertising...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Apply the regulatory roles of the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) in advertising practices and consumer protection, as well as the functions of the Copyright Office and Trademark Office in intellectual property rights and their application to graphic design and visual communications practices.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
