# B4.S003 - Demonstrate an understanding of printing operations on an offset press including...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate an understanding of printing operations on an offset press including prepress preparation, press setup, printing process, quality control, troubleshooting, maintenance and cleaning, and safety protocols.
