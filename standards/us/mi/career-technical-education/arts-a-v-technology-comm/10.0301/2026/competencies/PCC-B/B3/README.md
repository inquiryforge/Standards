# B3 - Demonstrate proficiency in prepress procedures encompassing resolution verification,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate proficiency in prepress procedures encompassing resolution verification, color correction, image manipulation, and file format selection, preparing and exporting documents or assets tailored for distinct printing processes.
