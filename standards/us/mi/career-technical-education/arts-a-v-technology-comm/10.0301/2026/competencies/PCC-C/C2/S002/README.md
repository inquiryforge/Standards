# C2.S002 - Demonstrate understanding and application of post-printing finishing techniques such...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate understanding and application of post-printing finishing techniques such as trimming, folding, perforating, gluing, stitching, binding, laminating, and wide-format specific techniques like mounting and grommeting.
