# B4.S005 - Explain the purpose and function of screen-printing equipment and materials, e.g.,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain the purpose and function of screen-printing equipment and materials, e.g., screens, emulsion, squeegees, inks, and demonstrate techniques such as flatbed, rotary, and garment printing.
