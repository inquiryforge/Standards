# A2.S017 - Demonstrate use of layers to manage design elements and complex projects, e.g.,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate use of layers to manage design elements and complex projects, e.g., adding, deleting, merging, flattening, hiding/showing, locking/unlocking, duplicating, renaming layers, and creating layer groups.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
