# B2.S003 - Explain and apply key steps in the design process: 1) Understand project purpose; 2)...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain and apply key steps in the design process: 1) Understand project purpose; 2) research and analyze; 3) establish goals and objectives; 4) brainstorm ideas; 5) develop sketches or prototypes; 6) refine and iterate; and 7) finalize and produce.
