# A3.S003 - Demonstrate plotter operation, including loading vinyl rolls, setting cutting...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate plotter operation, including loading vinyl rolls, setting cutting parameters, and sending designs for cutting, as well as the process of weeding and transferring vinyl decals onto substrates.
