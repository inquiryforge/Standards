# A2.S034 - Demonstrate manipulating graphics in frames including placing graphics into...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate manipulating graphics in frames including placing graphics into documents or into existing frames and using frames to affect visibility of the content.
