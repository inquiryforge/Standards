# A2.S002 - Explain how broadcast media’s role continues to evolve with advancements in...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain how broadcast media’s role continues to evolve with advancements in technology, changes in audience preferences, and the dynamic demands of entertainment consumption and evaluate how these advancements influence content creation, audience engagement, and industry practices.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
