# A1.S003 - Identify necessary Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) and ensure the correct use of...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify necessary Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) and ensure the correct use of appropriate protective gear, including gloves, eye protection, and ear protection, as needed, to effectively reduce workplace hazards.
