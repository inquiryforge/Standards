# A1.S005 - Examine standards related to noise exposure to protect broadcast media workers from...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Examine standards related to noise exposure to protect broadcast media workers from hearing loss and other health effects caused by prolonged exposure to elevated levels of noise.
