# A3.S015 - Describe elements and demonstrate the use of a camera and accessories, e.g.,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe elements and demonstrate the use of a camera and accessories, e.g., lens/optics, screen, eye piece, zoom control, focus control, menu navigation, recording media, battery/power adapter, and universal serial bus (USB) output.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
