# A3 - Apply video design principles and pre-production techniques to create compelling...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Apply video design principles and pre-production techniques to create compelling narratives for television, online streaming, and digital media platforms, while demonstrating technical proficiency in executing production plans to produce high-quality content.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
