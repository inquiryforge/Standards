# B2.S005 - Demonstrate use of a character generator (CG) system for real-time creation and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate use of a character generator (CG) system for real-time creation and insertion of graphics, lower-thirds, titles, and other text overlays into the video stream.
