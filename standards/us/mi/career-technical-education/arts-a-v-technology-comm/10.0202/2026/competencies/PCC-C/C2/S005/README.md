# C2.S005 - Demonstrate application of ethical principles and legal considerations specific to...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate application of ethical principles and legal considerations specific to broadcast journalism, including ensuring accuracy, fairness, objectivity, protecting privacy, avoiding defamation, and ethically handling confidential sources.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
