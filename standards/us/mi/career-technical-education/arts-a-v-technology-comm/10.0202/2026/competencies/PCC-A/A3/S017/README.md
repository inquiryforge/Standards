# A3.S017 - Demonstrate the use of various camera support equipment, such as monopods, tripods,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate the use of various camera support equipment, such as monopods, tripods, Steadicams, jibs, cranes, and matte boxes, including operating a camera with these tools to follow moving subjects while maintaining proper framing.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
