# A2.S001 - Analyze the role of multimedia broadcast professionals in the dissemination of news,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Analyze the role of multimedia broadcast professionals in the dissemination of news, information, and current affairs to the public and evaluate their impact and responsibilities in shaping public perception and understanding through various media platforms.
