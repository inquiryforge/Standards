# B4.S007 - Compare types of microphones, their pick-up patterns, and applications in a variety...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Compare types of microphones, their pick-up patterns, and applications in a variety of situations, e.g., omni-directional, cardioid, supercardioid, hypercardioid, bi-directional, unidirectional, shotgun, lavalier, and condenser.
