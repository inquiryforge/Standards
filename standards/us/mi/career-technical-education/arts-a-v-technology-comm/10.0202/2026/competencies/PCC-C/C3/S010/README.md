# C3.S010 - Refine an edited sequence/timeline by adjusting color balance, exposure, contrast,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Refine an edited sequence/timeline by adjusting color balance, exposure, contrast, saturation, and applying creative grading techniques to achieve desired visual effects, enhance mood, tone, and ensure visual consistency.
