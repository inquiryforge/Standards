# C3.S008 - Apply editing techniques to refine raw footage into a coherent narrative or...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Apply editing techniques to refine raw footage into a coherent narrative or presentation, including cuts, trims, color corrections, cropping, key framing, chroma key, transitions, compositing, continuity, and fades.
