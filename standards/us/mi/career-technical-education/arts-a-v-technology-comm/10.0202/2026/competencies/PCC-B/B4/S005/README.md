# B4.S005 - Identify components and describe the uses of an audio mixer to control and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify components and describe the uses of an audio mixer to control and manipulate recording and mixing stages of recording, e.g., input channels, volume controls, trim, equalizer (EQ) controls, pan pots, volume unit (VU) meters, cue channels, submixes, auxiliary channels, and output volume controls.
