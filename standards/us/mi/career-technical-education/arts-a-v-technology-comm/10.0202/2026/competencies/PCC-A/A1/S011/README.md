# A1.S011 - Demonstrate on-location safety practices in accordance with current industry safety...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate on-location safety practices in accordance with current industry safety standards, e.g., production equipment placement, cables and covering techniques, securing lighting equipment, and working with electrical sources.
