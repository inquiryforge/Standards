# C2.S009 - Create and publish content specifically for digital platforms, such as websites,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Create and publish content specifically for digital platforms, such as websites, social media platforms (like Twitter, Facebook, Instagram), and other online formats, adhering to the standards and practices of broadcast journalism.
