# B4.S002 - Apply measurement units, such as decibels (dB) for sound intensity and hertz (Hz)...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Apply measurement units, such as decibels (dB) for sound intensity and hertz (Hz) for frequency, to accurately assess and adjust audio levels and characteristics.
