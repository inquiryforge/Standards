# B2.S009 - Identify basic video connectors, such as Bayonet Neill-Concelman (BNC), F-connector,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify basic video connectors, such as Bayonet Neill-Concelman (BNC), F-connector, VGA, DVI, RCA, Y/C (S-Video), and HDMI, and differentiate their uses in video transmission and display technologies.
