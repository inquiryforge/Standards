# C2 - Evaluate and create media content across various platforms and formats, including...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Evaluate and create media content across various platforms and formats, including digital, broadcast, and social media, by applying journalistic principles, production techniques, and ethical standards.
