# B2.S002 - Demonstrate use of a playback/recording device to play back pre-recorded content and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate use of a playback/recording device to play back pre-recorded content and record live footage, including digital video recorders (DVRs) and solid-state recorders.
