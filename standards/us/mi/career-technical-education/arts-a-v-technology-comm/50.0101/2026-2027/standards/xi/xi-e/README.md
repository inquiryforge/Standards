# XI.E - Demonstrate (alone and with others) vocal or instrumental performance that includes...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate (alone and with others) vocal or instrumental performance that includes a varied repertoire of music to show competence with fundamental elements used in the art form.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
