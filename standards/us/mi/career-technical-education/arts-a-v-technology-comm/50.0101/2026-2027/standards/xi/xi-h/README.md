# XI.H - Analyze the physical, emotional, and social dimensions of characters found in...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Analyze the physical, emotional, and social dimensions of characters found in dramatic texts from various styles and media to acquire an understanding of key issues affecting the creation of characters included in texts.
