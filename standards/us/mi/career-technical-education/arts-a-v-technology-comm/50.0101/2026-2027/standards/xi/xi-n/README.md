# XI.N - Research past and present production stage sets, costumes, lighting, and other...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Research past and present production stage sets, costumes, lighting, and other effects that support a performance in order to build an understanding regarding the use of technical aspects to enhance productions.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
