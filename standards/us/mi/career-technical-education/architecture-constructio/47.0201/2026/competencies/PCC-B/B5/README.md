# B5 - Demonstrate the ability to interpret and analyze a variety of drawings and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate the ability to interpret and analyze a variety of drawings and literature utilized in HVACR such as but not limited to: blueprints, schematics, sequence of operation from schematics, and CAD documents.
