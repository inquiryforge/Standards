# F1 - Employ an understanding of the various types of hairdressing techniques and their...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Employ an understanding of the various types of hairdressing techniques and their proficient application in order to provide a safe, efficient hairdressing service with skill suitable for employment in the salon. (Competencies repeated for dressing, artificial hair, natural hair cultivation, wet hair styling, and arranging.)
<!-- standard-text:end -->
