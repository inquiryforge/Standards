# A6 - Employ acquired knowledge of Applied Anatomy, Physiology, and Histology of the human...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Employ acquired knowledge of Applied Anatomy, Physiology, and Histology of the human head, hands, nails, skin and hair as it relates to each service area in order to provide a safe and efficient service for a client.
