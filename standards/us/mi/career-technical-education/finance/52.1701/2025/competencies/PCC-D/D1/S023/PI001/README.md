# D1.S023.PI001 - Describe filed inland marine insurance coverages, including commercial articles,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe filed inland marine insurance coverages, including commercial articles, camera and musical instrument dealers, equipment dealers, physicians and surgeons equipment, signs theatrical property, film, floor plans, jewelers blocks, mails, accounts receivable, and valuable papers and records.
