# C2.S005 - Assess liquidity and solvency by calculating and interpreting financial ratios...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Assess liquidity and solvency by calculating and interpreting financial ratios (e.g., working capital, current ratio, quick ratio, cash ratio, inventory turnover, accounts receivable turnover, operating cycle).
