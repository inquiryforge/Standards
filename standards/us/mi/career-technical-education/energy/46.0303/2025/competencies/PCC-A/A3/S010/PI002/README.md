# A3.S010.PI002 - Explain the impact of rapid expansion of electricity service including how much...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain the impact of rapid expansion of electricity service including how much electricity modern appliances and devices consume, how the Rural Electrification Act impacted Americans in rural areas, and how electrification can help limit the amount of carbon released into the atmosphere.
