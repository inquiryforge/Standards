# C1.S009.PI001 - Describe electric transmission system challenges and opportunities, including...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe electric transmission system challenges and opportunities, including extreme weather events, infrastructure maintenance and why expanding the transmission system is essential for adding more renewable energy generation to the utility grid.
