# D1.S001 - Research and evaluate records and reports regarding the safety, health and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Research and evaluate records and reports regarding the safety, health and environmental performance in a workplace to identify potential hazards and workplace problems common to law, public safety, security and corrections professions.
