# A1.S002 - Assess and implement methods to reduce sources of office, facility and mobile...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Assess and implement methods to reduce sources of office, facility and mobile operations accident hazards common in the law, public safety, security and corrections industry in order to promote a safe and accident free working environment.
