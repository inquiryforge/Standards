# A1.S001 - Complete work tasks in accordance with applicable employer rules concerning...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Complete work tasks in accordance with applicable employer rules concerning occupational safety and health common to the law, public safety, security and corrections industry in order to maintain employee rights and employer obligations.
