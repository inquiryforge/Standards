# A3.S001 - Demonstrate the effective use of computer based equipment (containing embedded...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate the effective use of computer based equipment (containing embedded computers, or processors) in order to control electromechanical devices commonly used in conducting work within the law and public safety practice.
