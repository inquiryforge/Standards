# D4.S001.PI001 - Analyze the various roles of leaders within organizations (e.g. contribute ideas;...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Analyze the various roles of leaders within organizations (e.g. contribute ideas; share in building an organization; act as role models to employees by adhering to company policies, procedures, and standards; promote the organization’s vision; and mentor others).
