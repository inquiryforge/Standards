# A3 - Implement personal & jobsite safety skills and safe disposal of materials as...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Implement personal & jobsite safety skills and safe disposal of materials as recognized by industry standards and government agencies, and take corrective action as needed.
