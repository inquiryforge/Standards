# B1.S003 - Maintain laboratory records documented in a laboratory to ensure data accuracy and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Maintain laboratory records documented in a laboratory to ensure data accuracy and integrity (e.g., avoid bias, record any conflicts of interest, avoid misinterpreted results, etc.).
