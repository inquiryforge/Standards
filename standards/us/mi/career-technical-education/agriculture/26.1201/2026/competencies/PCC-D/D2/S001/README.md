# D2.S001 - Demonstrate the application of biotechnology to solve problems in Agriculture, Food...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate the application of biotechnology to solve problems in Agriculture, Food and Natural Resources (AFNR) systems (e.g., bioengineering, food processing, waste management, horticulture, forestry, livestock, crops, etc.).
