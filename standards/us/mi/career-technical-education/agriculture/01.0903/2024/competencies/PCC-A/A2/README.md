# A2 - Follow and implement job site safety rules and industry regulations to maintain safe...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Follow and implement job site safety rules and industry regulations to maintain safe and healthful working conditions for people, animals, and the environment, including consideration of preventing the transmission of zoonotic diseases.
