# III - PATIENT AND OTHER CLIENT MOVEMENT - DIAGNOSTIC SERVICES PROFESSIONALS WILL...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
PATIENT AND OTHER CLIENT MOVEMENT - DIAGNOSTIC SERVICES PROFESSIONALS WILL UNDERSTAND THE PRINCIPLES OF BODY MECHANICS FOR POSITIONING, TRANSFERRING, AND TRANSPORTING OF PATIENTS AND OTHER CLIENTS. THESE ACTIVITIES WILL BE PERFORMED EFFICIENTLY WITHOUT INJURY TO PATIENTS AND OTHER CLIENTS OR SELF.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
