# IV.A.3 - Prepare a serial dilution of a microbial culture starting with 10-3 going to 10-8...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Prepare a serial dilution of a microbial culture starting with 10-3 going to 10-8 and plate on to nutrient agar petri dishes. Determine the original concentration of the microbial culture.
