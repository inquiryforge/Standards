# II - ACADEMIC FOUNDATIONS Biotechnology R & D professionals will be knowledgeable in the...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
ACADEMIC FOUNDATIONS Biotechnology R & D professionals will be knowledgeable in the fundamentals of mathematical concepts, statistics, genetics, organic chemistry, biochemistry, cell biology, molecular biology and microbiology.
