# III - INTRODUCTION TO BIOTECHNOLOGY KNOWLEDGE AREAS AND TECHNIQUES Biotechnology R & D...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
INTRODUCTION TO BIOTECHNOLOGY KNOWLEDGE AREAS AND TECHNIQUES Biotechnology R & D professionals will be introduced to the following recombinant DNA and genetic engineering, bioprocessing (producing recombinant DNA products on a large scale for profit), monoclonal antibody production, separation and purification of biotechnology products, nanotechnology, bioinformatics, genomics, proteomics and transcriptomics.
