# I - CONTRIBUTIONS OF BIOTECHNOLOGY TO HEALTH AND HUMAN CONDITION Biotechnology R & D...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
CONTRIBUTIONS OF BIOTECHNOLOGY TO HEALTH AND HUMAN CONDITION Biotechnology R & D professionals will understand that biotechnology products are based on molecular biology of disease and health; the quality of life through finding a cure for genetic, environmental and behavioral diseases, chronic conditions, industrial enzymes and new diagnostic tools; and legal and ethical issues to protect and preserve the quality of life, with emphasis on social and diversity issues.
