# B1.S003 - Therapeutic services professionals will be able to explain planned procedures and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Therapeutic services professionals will be able to explain planned procedures and goals to patients and other clients. They will use various strategies to respond to questions and concerns of patients and other clients.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
