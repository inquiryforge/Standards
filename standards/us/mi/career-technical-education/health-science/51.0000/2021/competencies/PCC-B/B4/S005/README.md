# B4.S005 - Therapeutic services professionals will understand the general purpose and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Therapeutic services professionals will understand the general purpose and components of the treatment plan. They will collaborate in planning procedures according to facility protocol and regulatory guidelines. They will understand how these procedures support the goals and objectives of the treatment plan of the patient or other client and implement the procedures within their scope of practice.
