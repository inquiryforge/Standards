# B2.S013.PI001 - Correctly identify and document causes of malfunctions in mechanical components or...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Correctly identify and document causes of malfunctions in mechanical components or electrical drives, based upon the technical documentation, and where possible correct the malfunctions or correctly identify the expertise required to correct the malfunction.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
