# A1.S003.PI001 - a. Identify appropriate infection control procedures and assess strategies/methods...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
a. Identify appropriate infection control procedures and assess strategies/methods to control risks. b. Describe universal precautions for blood-borne pathogens and the procedures for responding to and reporting exposure. c. Explain procedures related to recognition of and responses to controlled substance risks. d. Describe safe body mechanics relevant to the learning setting. e. Describe safe ergonomics relevant to the learning setting. f. Explain procedures related to criminal background checks, if appropriate to the learning setting. g. Identify governmental regulations regarding worker and learner safety. h. Describe fire precautions and response procedures. i. Describe procedures for responding to violent and threatening behaviors, health risks and fire emergencies. j. Describe procedures for documenting and reporting violent and threatening behaviors, health risks and fire emergencies. k. Identify risks to psychological health in the learning setting. l. Explain appropriate responses to psychological risks. m. Monitor compliance with relevant individual health and safety regulations and procedures. n. Identify types of bullying and develop appropriate strategies/methods for dealing with bullying.
