# A5 - Demonstrate the operation of lodging facilities and the care of guests who use those...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate the operation of lodging facilities and the care of guests who use those facilities through direct guest contact or the provision of background services that enhance the guest experience.
