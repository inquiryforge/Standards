# PI045 - HT-LOD 8.1: Identify the different types of reservations used to meet different...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
HT-LOD 8.1: Identify the different types of reservations used to meet different guest needs · Distinguish guaranteed from non- guaranteed reservations. · Contrast reservations guaranteed by credit card with reservations guaranteed by travel agents or corporations. · Contrast advanced deposits with prepaid reservations.
