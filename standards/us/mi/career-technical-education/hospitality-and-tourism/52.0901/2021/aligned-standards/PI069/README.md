# PI069 - HT-REC 5.2: Identify sources of assistance to utilize in emergency situations for...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
HT-REC 5.2: Identify sources of assistance to utilize in emergency situations for self, co-workers and customers/guests. · Detail three resources for assistance with communication. · Name the most common source for criminal assistance. · Name a resource for assistance with environmental issues. · List a source for assistance with legal issues. · List two sources for assistance with medical or social services.
