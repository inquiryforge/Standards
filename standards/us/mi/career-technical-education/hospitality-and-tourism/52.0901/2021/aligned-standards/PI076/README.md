# PI076 - HT-REC 8.4: Summarize the policies and procedures used to ensure guest safety. ·...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
HT-REC 8.4: Summarize the policies and procedures used to ensure guest safety. · Describe policies about items such as backpacks, strollers, wheelchairs, packages, high heels, etc., that would minimize damage and safety in an attraction or amusement setting. · Identify emergency evacuation routes.
