# PI098 - HT-TT 3.4: Research cultural expectations of other areas, regions and countries to...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
HT-TT 3.4: Research cultural expectations of other areas, regions and countries to help avoid social improprieties. · Identify cultures where eye contact is not accepted. · Identify cultures where you should not pat on a child on the head. · Identify cultures where you cannot offer food with your left hand. · Identify cultures where you should not show the bottom of your feet.
