# PI016 - HT- 6.2: Match personal interests and aptitudes to careers in the hospitality and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
HT- 6.2: Match personal interests and aptitudes to careers in the hospitality and tourism industry when researching opportunities within the pathways. Identify personal interests and aptitudes. Identify job requirements and characteristics of selected careers. Compare personal interests and aptitudes with job requirements and characteristics of career selected. Modify career goals based on results of personal interests and aptitudes with career requirements and characteristics.
