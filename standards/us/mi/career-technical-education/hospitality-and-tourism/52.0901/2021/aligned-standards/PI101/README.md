# PI101 - HT-TT 5.1: Formulate methods of resolution and/or alternatives to eliminate...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
HT-TT 5.1: Formulate methods of resolution and/or alternatives to eliminate potential safety hazards. · Create a proactive solution to address common safety hazards including lighting, sound, surface areas and political and social climate. · Create a reactive solution to guest exposure to a health hazard. · Prepare alternate methods of reaching travel or experience objective(s). · Suggest ways to manage guests and groups facing safety hazards. · Develop and role-play mock emergency situations demonstrating caution and good judgment.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
