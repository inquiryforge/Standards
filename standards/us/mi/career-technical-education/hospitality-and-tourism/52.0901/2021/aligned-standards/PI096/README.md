# PI096 - HT-TT 3.2: Summarize the significance of body language and personal space in...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
HT-TT 3.2: Summarize the significance of body language and personal space in different cultures. · Identify at least three common hand gestures and body language positions unacceptable in another culture. · Show the personal space preferred during conversations by cultures such as Asians, Middle Easterners, Latin Americans and North Americans.
