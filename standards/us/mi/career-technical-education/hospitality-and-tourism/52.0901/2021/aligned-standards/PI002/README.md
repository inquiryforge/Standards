# PI002 - HT- 1.2: Identify effects of the economy on the hospitality and tourism industry to...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
HT- 1.2: Identify effects of the economy on the hospitality and tourism industry to effectively plan products and services. Describe how economics applies to the hospitality and tourism industry. Discuss the importance of the hospitality and tourism industry to the U.S. economy. Explain the effects that supply and demand have on the hospitality and tourism industry.
