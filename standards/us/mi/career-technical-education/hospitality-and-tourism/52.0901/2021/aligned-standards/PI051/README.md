# PI051 - HT-LOD 10.1: Describe the role of a cashier in facilitating cash control. ·...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
HT-LOD 10.1: Describe the role of a cashier in facilitating cash control. · Demonstrate use of cash sheets to record cash collected. · Demonstrate use of cash sheets to record cash paid out. · Explain how cash banks are issued to cashiers. · Describe how cashiers turn in cash banks and prepare cash deposits. · Explain how cash banks and cashier cash deposits are reconciled.
