# PI040 - HT-LOD 7.4: Explain how methods of payment are established with arriving guests to...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
HT-LOD 7.4: Explain how methods of payment are established with arriving guests to clarify payment procedures. · Describe common payment methods for arriving guests. · Demonstrate procedure for accepting payment by check. · Demonstrate procedure for accepting payment by credit card. · Describe special payment methods.
