# PI126 - HT-RFB 1.2: Comply with laws regarding hiring, harassment and safety issues. ·...

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
HT-RFB 1.2: Comply with laws regarding hiring, harassment and safety issues. · Demonstrate understanding of legal interviewing processes. · Outline how harassment and stereotyping can create an unhealthy work environment. · Define legal and ethical responsibilities for safety procedures. · Interpret worker's compensation requirements and forms.
