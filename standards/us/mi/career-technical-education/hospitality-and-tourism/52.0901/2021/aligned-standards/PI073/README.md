# PI073 - HT-REC 8.1: Prepare introductory information about a facility to distribute to...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
HT-REC 8.1: Prepare introductory information about a facility to distribute to guests. · List all locations a guest would need to know. · Describe the guest use of each area. · Identify the schedule opening and closing for each area of the facility (days/seasons). · Give a short history or background of the facility if relevant.
