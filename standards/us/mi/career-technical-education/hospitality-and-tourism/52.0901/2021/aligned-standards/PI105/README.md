# PI105 - HT-TT 6.2: Utilize travel and tourism terms appropriately to guide customizing...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
HT-TT 6.2: Utilize travel and tourism terms appropriately to guide customizing services for guests. · Explain differences between meal plan options. · Distinguish differences between tour guides and escorts and group leaders. · Explain differences between FIT and group travel. · Summarize room night, inside cabin, berth, vacation, itinerary, day trip, etc. in different segments (cruises, tour planning, meeting planner and others).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
