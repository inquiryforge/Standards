# ELD-SC.6-8.Argue.Interpretive - Interpret scientific arguments by ● Identifying convincing evidence from data,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Interpret scientific arguments by ● Identifying convincing evidence from data, models, and/or information from investigations of phenomena or design solutions ● Comparing reasoning and claims based on evidence from two arguments on the same topic ● Evaluating whether they emphasize similar or different evidence and/or interpretations of facts Language Functions and Sample Language
