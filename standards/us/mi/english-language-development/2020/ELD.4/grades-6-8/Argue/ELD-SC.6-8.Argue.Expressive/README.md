# ELD-SC.6-8.Argue.Expressive - Construct scientific arguments that ● Introduce and contextualize topic/ phenomenon...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Construct scientific arguments that ● Introduce and contextualize topic/ phenomenon in issues related to the natural and designed world(s) ● Support or refute a claim based on data and evidence ● Establish and maintain a neutral or objective stance ● Signal logical relationships among reasoning, evidence, data, and/or a model when making or defending a claim or counterclaim Features
<!-- standard-text:end -->
