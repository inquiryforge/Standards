# ELD-SC.9-12.Argue.Expressive - Construct scientific arguments that ● Introduce and contextualize topic/ phenomenon...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Construct scientific arguments that ● Introduce and contextualize topic/ phenomenon in current scientific or historical episodes in science ● Defend or refute a claim based on data and evidence ● Establish and maintain an appropriate tone and stance (neutral/objective or biased/ subjective) ● Signal logical relationships among reasoning, evidence, data, and/or models when making and defending a claim, counterclaim, and/or rebuttal Features
<!-- standard-text:end -->
