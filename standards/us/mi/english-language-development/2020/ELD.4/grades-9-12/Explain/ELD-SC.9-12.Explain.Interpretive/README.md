# ELD-SC.9-12.Explain.Interpretive - Interpret scientific explanations by ● Defining investigable questions or problems...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Interpret scientific explanations by ● Defining investigable questions or problems based on observations, information, and/or data about a phenomenon ● Paraphrasing central ideas in complex evidence, concepts, processes, and information to help explain how or why a phenomenon occurs ● Evaluating the extent to which reasoning, theory and/or models link evidence to claims and support conclusions Language Functions and Sample Language
<!-- standard-text:end -->
