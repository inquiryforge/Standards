# ELD-SC.1.Inform.Expressive - Construct scientific informational texts that ● Introduce others to topic or entity...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Construct scientific informational texts that ● Introduce others to topic or entity ● Define, describe, and classify concept, topic, or entity ● Summarize observations or factual information Features
