# ELD-SI.4-12.Argue - ● Generate questions about different perspectives ● Support or challenge an opinion,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
● Generate questions about different perspectives ● Support or challenge an opinion, premise, or interpretation ● Clarify and elaborate ideas based on feedback ● Evaluate changes in thinking, identifying trade-offs ● Refine claims and reasoning based on new information or evidence
