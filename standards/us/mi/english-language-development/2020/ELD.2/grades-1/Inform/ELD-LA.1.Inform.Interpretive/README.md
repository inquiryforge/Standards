# ELD-LA.1.Inform.Interpretive - Interpret informational texts in language C arts by a ● Identifying main topic...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Interpret informational texts in language C arts by a ● Identifying main topic and/or entity and ● key details ● Asking and answering questions ● about descriptions of attributes and characteristics ● Identifying word choices in relation to topic or content area Language Functions and Sample Language
