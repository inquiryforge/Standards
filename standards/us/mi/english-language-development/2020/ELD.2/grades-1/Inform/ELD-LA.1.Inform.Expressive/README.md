# ELD-LA.1.Inform.Expressive - Construct informational texts in language arts that ● Introduce and define topic...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Construct informational texts in language arts that ● Introduce and define topic and/or entity for audience ● Describe attributes and characteristics with facts, definitions, and relevant details Features
