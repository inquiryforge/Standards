# ELD-LA.6-8.Inform.Interpretive - Interpret informational texts in language Co arts by ● ● Identifying and/or...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Interpret informational texts in language Co arts by ● ● Identifying and/or summarizing main ideas and their relationship to ● supporting ideas ● ● Analyzing observations and descriptions in textual evidence for key attributes, qualities, characteristics, activities, and behaviors ● ● Evaluating the impact of author’s key word choices over the course of a text Language Functions and Sample Language
<!-- standard-text:end -->
