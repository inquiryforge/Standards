# ELD-LA.9-12.Inform.Expressive - Construct informational texts in language arts that ● Introduce and define topic...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Construct informational texts in language arts that ● Introduce and define topic and/or entity for audience ● Establish an objective or neutral stance ● Add precision, details, and clarity about complex attributes, qualities, characteristics, activities, and conceptual relationships ● Develop coherence and cohesion throughout text Features
<!-- standard-text:end -->
