# ELD-LA.9-12.Inform.Interpretive - Interpret informational texts in language arts by ● Identifying and/or summarizing...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Interpret informational texts in language arts by ● Identifying and/or summarizing central ideas ● Analyzing descriptions and inferences in textual evidence for key attributes, qualities, characteristics, activities, and conceptual relationships ● Evaluating cumulative impact and refinement of author’s key word choices over the course of text Language Functions and Sample Language
<!-- standard-text:end -->
