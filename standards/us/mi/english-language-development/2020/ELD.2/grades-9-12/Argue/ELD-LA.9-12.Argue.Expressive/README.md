# ELD-LA.9-12.Argue.Expressive - Construct language arts arguments that ● Introduce and develop precise claims and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Construct language arts arguments that ● Introduce and develop precise claims and address counterclaims ● Support claims and refute counterclaims with valid reasoning and relevant and sufficient evidence ● Establish and maintain a formal style and objective tone ● Logically organize claims, counterclaims, reasons, and evidence; offer a conclusion with recommendations Features
<!-- standard-text:end -->
