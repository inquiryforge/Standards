# ELD-LA.9-12.Argue.Interpretive - Interpret language arts arguments by ● Identifying and summarizing central ideas of...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Interpret language arts arguments by ● Identifying and summarizing central ideas of primary or secondary sources ● Analyzing use of rhetoric and details to advance point of view or purpose ● Evaluating and corroborating relevance and sufficiency of evidence as well as validity of reasoning to support claims Language Functions and Sample Language
