# ELD-MA.K.Inform.Expressive - Construct mathematical informational texts (with prompting and support) that ●...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Construct mathematical informational texts (with prompting and support) that ● Define or classify concept or entity ● Describe a concept or entity ● Compare/contrast concepts or entities Features
