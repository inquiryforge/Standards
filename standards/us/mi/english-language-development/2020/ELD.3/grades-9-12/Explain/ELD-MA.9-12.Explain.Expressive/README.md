# ELD-MA.9-12.Explain.Expressive - Construct mathematical explanation s that ● Introduce mathematical concept or entity...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Construct mathematical explanation s that ● Introduce mathematical concept or entity ● Share solutions with others ● Describe data and/or approach used to solve a problem ● State reasoning used to generate own or alternate solutions Features
