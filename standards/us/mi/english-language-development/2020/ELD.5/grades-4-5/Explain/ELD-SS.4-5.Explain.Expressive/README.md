# ELD-SS.4-5.Explain.Expressive - Construct social studies explanations that ● Introduce phenomena or events ●...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Construct social studies explanations that ● Introduce phenomena or events ● Describe components, order, causes and effects, or cycles using relevant examples and details ● Generalize probable causes and effects of developments or events Features
