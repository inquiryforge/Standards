# ELD-SS.6-8.Argue.Expressive - Construct social studies arguments that ● Introduce and contextualize topic ● Select...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Construct social studies arguments that ● Introduce and contextualize topic ● Select relevant information to support claims with evidence gathered from multiple sources ● Establish perspective ● Show relationships between claims and counterclaims, differences in perspectives, and evidence and reasoning Features
