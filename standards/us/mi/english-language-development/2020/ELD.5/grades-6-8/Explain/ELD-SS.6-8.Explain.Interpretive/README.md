# ELD-SS.6-8.Explain.Interpretive - Interpret social studies explanations by ● Determining multiple points of view in...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Interpret social studies explanations by ● Determining multiple points of view in sources for answering compelling and supporting questions about phenomena or events ● Analyzing sources for logical relationships among contributing factors or causes ● Evaluating experts’ points of agreement, along with strengths and weakness of explanations Language Functions and Sample Language
