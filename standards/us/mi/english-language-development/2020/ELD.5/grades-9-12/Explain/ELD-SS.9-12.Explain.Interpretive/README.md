# ELD-SS.9-12.Explain.Interpretive - Interpret social studies explanations by ● Determining multiple types of sources,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Interpret social studies explanations by ● Determining multiple types of sources, points of view in sources, and potential uses of sources for answering compelling and supporting questions about phenomena or events ● Analyzing sources for logical relationships among contributing factors, causes, or related concepts ● Evaluating experts’ points of agreement and disagreement based on their consistency with explanation given its purpose Language Functions and Sample Language
<!-- standard-text:end -->
