# ELD-SS.9-12.Explain.Expressive - Construct social studies explanations that ● Introduce and contextualize multiple...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Construct social studies explanations that ● Introduce and contextualize multiple phenomena or events ● Establish perspective for communicating intended and unintended outcomes, consequences, or documentation ● Develop sound reasoning, sequences with linear and nonlinear relationships, evidence, and details with significant and pertinent information, acknowledging strengths and weaknesses ● Generalize experts’ points of agreement and disagreement about multiple, complex causes and effects of developments or events Features
<!-- standard-text:end -->
