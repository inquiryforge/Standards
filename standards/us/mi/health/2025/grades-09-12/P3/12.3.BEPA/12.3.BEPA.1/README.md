# 12.3.BEPA.1 - Evaluate the validity of claims made by companies and social media influencers...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Evaluate the validity of claims made by companies and social media influencers intended to promote or improve nutrition, fitness, and a healthy, active lifestyle.
