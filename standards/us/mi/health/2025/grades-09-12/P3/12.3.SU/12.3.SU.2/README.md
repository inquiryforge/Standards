# 12.3.SU.2 - Use valid and reliable information to analyze the relationship between the use of...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Use valid and reliable information to analyze the relationship between the use of legal and illegal (regarding legal age of use and type) substances and the causes of death (including overdose) and disease (including addiction) in the United States.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
