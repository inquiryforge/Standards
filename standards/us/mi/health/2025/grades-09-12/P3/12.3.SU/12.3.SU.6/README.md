# 12.3.SU.6 - Access valid and reliable resources to determine laws regarding the purchase,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Access valid and reliable resources to determine laws regarding the purchase, distribution, and selling of substances (e.g., dispensaries, on the street, stores, legal vs. illegal, travel across state lines).
