# 12.3.SU.1 - Evaluate the validity of information, products, services, and resources intended to...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Evaluate the validity of information, products, services, and resources intended to help a person make health-promoting choices when making decisions related to legal substance use.
