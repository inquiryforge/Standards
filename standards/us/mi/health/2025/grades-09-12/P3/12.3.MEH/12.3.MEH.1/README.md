# 12.3.MEH.1 - Demonstrate how to access valid and reliable mental health information and resources...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate how to access valid and reliable mental health information and resources to help or support someone who has experienced harassment, abuse, assault, discrimination, and/or exploitation.
