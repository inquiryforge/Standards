# 12.3.HR.10 - Use assertive communication techniques, including refusals, in a variety of settings...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Use assertive communication techniques, including refusals, in a variety of settings and with a variety of audiences to meet personal needs and maintain or enhance overall health.
