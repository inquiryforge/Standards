# 12.3.HR.1 - Explain the harm that can be caused by disrespecting others with differing views and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain the harm that can be caused by disrespecting others with differing views and beliefs, and demonstrate positive ways to express understanding of differing perspectives.
