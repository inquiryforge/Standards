# 12.1.CEH.4 - Analyze the behavioral (e.g., sedentary lifestyle, smoking, dietary habits) and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Analyze the behavioral (e.g., sedentary lifestyle, smoking, dietary habits) and environmental factors (e.g., policies, access and availability, built environment) that contribute to major chronic diseases (e.g., diabetes, heart disease, lung cancer).
