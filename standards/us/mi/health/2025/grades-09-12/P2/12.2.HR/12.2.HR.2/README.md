# 12.2.HR.2 - Use effective strategies (e.g., boundary setting, maintaining personal boundaries,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Use effective strategies (e.g., boundary setting, maintaining personal boundaries, respecting others’ boundaries, I-statements, stating your needs, recognizing warning signs) to leave negative relationships and improve or maintain positive relationships.
