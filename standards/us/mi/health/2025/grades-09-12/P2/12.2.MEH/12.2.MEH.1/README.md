# 12.2.MEH.1 - Describe positive (e.g., developmental assets, protective factors, resilience,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe positive (e.g., developmental assets, protective factors, resilience, supportive adult relationships) and negative factors (e.g., trauma, adversity) that can affect mental and emotional health and well-being.
