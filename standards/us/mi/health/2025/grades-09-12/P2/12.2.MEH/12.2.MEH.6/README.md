# 12.2.MEH.6 - Analyze power imbalances in relationships and demonstrate strategies that...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Analyze power imbalances in relationships and demonstrate strategies that communicate your values, your right to say no, and your ability to hold others accountable for their actions.
