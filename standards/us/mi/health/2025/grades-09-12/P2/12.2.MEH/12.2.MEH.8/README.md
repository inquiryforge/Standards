# 12.2.MEH.8 - Communicate when there is a concern about one’s own or someone else’s mental...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Communicate when there is a concern about one’s own or someone else’s mental well-being or when someone is considering self-harm or suicide.
