# 12.5.MEH.2 - Apply coping and stress management techniques to manage a variety of stressors...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Apply coping and stress management techniques to manage a variety of stressors (e.g., school, personal life, relationships) and create a long-term plan for stress management.
