# 12.5.BEPA.9 - Determine a variety of physical activities that can be implemented independently or...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Determine a variety of physical activities that can be implemented independently or with minimal support for the purposes of personal enjoyment or challenge, or to maintain or improve fitness.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
