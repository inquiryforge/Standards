# 12.6.CEH.3 - Develop and/or implement a plan for participating in projects to help make positive...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Develop and/or implement a plan for participating in projects to help make positive changes in a community (e.g., volunteering, linking with experts in the community, service-learning, service project).
