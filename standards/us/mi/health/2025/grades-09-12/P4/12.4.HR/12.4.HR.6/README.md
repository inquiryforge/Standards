# 12.4.HR.6 - Evaluate strategies (e.g., setting expectations and/or boundaries) for dealing with...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Evaluate strategies (e.g., setting expectations and/or boundaries) for dealing with difficult relationships with family members, peers, and partners and demonstrate the ability to use these strategies to make health-promoting decisions.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
