# 12.4.HR.8 - Demonstrate the ability to apply a thoughtful decision-making process to maintain or...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate the ability to apply a thoughtful decision-making process to maintain or enhance relationship health, including the decision to leave or seek help in an unhealthy relationship.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
