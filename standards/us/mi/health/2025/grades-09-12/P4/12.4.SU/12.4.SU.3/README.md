# 12.4.SU.3 - Analyze the potential short- and long-term impacts of legal and illegal substances...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Analyze the potential short- and long-term impacts of legal and illegal substances (e.g., opioids) on multiple dimensions of health Michigan Health Education Standards Guidelines 2025 51 (e.g., physical, mental, emotional, social, and/or intellectual) and on other health risk behaviors (e.g., sexual activity, impaired driving, use of other drugs).
