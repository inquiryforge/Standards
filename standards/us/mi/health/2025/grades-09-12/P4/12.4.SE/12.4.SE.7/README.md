# 12.4.SE.7 - Explain age of consent laws and examine the various components of consent (e.g.,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain age of consent laws and examine the various components of consent (e.g., consent must be asked for and verbally given, consent cannot be given if under the influence, consent can be taken away at any time).
