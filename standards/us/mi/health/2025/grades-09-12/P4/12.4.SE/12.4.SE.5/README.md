# 12.4.SE.5 - Evaluate readiness, options, and their respective outcomes regarding decisions about...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Evaluate readiness, options, and their respective outcomes regarding decisions about whether to engage in sexual activity, including abstaining, postponing sexual intercourse, engaging in risk reduction practices (e.g., using condoms and other barriers, using birth control).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
