# 2.4.BEPA.10 - Identify physical activity as a health-promoting habit that contributes to overall...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify physical activity as a health-promoting habit that contributes to overall health and well-being and list the benefits of these habits on physical well-being (e.g., activities that strengthen the heart and cardiovascular systems, contribute to fitness and muscle-building) and mental health (e.g., stress management).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
