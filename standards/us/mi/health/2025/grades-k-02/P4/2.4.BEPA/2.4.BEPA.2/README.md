# 2.4.BEPA.2 - Identify, with adult guidance, food-related decisions that can be made independently...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify, with adult guidance, food-related decisions that can be made independently (e.g., asking for healthier options) or when assistance is needed (e.g., managing a food allergy).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
