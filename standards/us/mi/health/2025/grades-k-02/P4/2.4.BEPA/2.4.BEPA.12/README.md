# 2.4.BEPA.12 - Set a short-term physical activity goal relevant to specific needs and abilities,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Set a short-term physical activity goal relevant to specific needs and abilities, take meaningful action toward achieving the goal, and identify people at home or at school who can help when assistance is needed to achieve the goal.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
