# 2.4.BEPA.11 - Identify opportunities, in and out of the school setting, for safe, active play, and...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify opportunities, in and out of the school setting, for safe, active play, and physical activity for self-expression, social interaction, personal enjoyment, and challenge.
