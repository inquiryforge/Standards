# 2.3.SU.3 - Identify trusted adults at home and school who can discuss rules and practices...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify trusted adults at home and school who can discuss rules and practices related to medicine use (e.g., only taking medicine with an adult’s help, only taking prescriptions prescribed to you).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
