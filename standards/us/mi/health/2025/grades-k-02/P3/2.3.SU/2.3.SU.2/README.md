# 2.3.SU.2 - Explain the importance of using prescription and over-the-counter medications...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Explain the importance of using prescription and over-the-counter medications correctly and safely and the potential risks associated with misusing and/or ingesting household products.
