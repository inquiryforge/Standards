# 2.3.PHW.2 - Identify individuals who can assist with health-related issues and potentially...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify individuals who can assist with health-related issues and potentially life-threatening health conditions (e.g., asthma episodes, diabetes, allergic reactions, seizures, concussions).
