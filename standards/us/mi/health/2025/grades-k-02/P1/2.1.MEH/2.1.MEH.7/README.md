# 2.1.MEH.7 - Articulate and celebrate the individual characteristics that make a person unique...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Articulate and celebrate the individual characteristics that make a person unique and explain that different factors (e.g., peers, media, culture, family, phase of life, etc.) can influence how a person views themselves.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
