# 2.1.MEH.8 - Demonstrate strategies that help all students feel welcome and valued as a part of...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate strategies that help all students feel welcome and valued as a part of the school community (e.g., cooperative playing, listening, showing you care, sharing).
