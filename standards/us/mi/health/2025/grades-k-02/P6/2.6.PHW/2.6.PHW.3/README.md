# 2.6.PHW.3 - Recognize how the actions of others can affect physical health (e.g., spreading...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Recognize how the actions of others can affect physical health (e.g., spreading germs) and encourage peers to make positive choices about physical health habits and prevention strategies.
