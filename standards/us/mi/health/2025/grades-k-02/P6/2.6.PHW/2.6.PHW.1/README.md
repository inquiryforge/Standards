# 2.6.PHW.1 - Describe personal health habits (e.g., brushing and flossing teeth, hand washing,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe personal health habits (e.g., brushing and flossing teeth, hand washing, bathing and/or showering, sufficient sleep, sun safety, physical activity, limiting screen time) that can prevent illness and promote self-care and overall health.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
