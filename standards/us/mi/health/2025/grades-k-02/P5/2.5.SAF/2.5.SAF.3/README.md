# 2.5.SAF.3 - Demonstrate how to respond (e.g., yell, get away, tell an adult, seek help) and get...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate how to respond (e.g., yell, get away, tell an adult, seek help) and get help in various emergency situations including when and how to call 9-1-1.
