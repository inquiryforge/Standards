# 2.5.MEH.1 - Demonstrate self-control (e.g., delay gratification, wait your turn) independently...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate self-control (e.g., delay gratification, wait your turn) independently or with the support of adults in various settings (e.g., on the playground, in the classroom, during physical education, at an assembly).
