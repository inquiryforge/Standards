# 2.5.PHW.4 - Demonstrate independence in health-promoting practices such as hand washing, tooth...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate independence in health-promoting practices such as hand washing, tooth brushing, sneezing and coughing, disposal of tissues, and physical activity.
