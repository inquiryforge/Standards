# 8.4.SAF.4 - Describe laws (e.g., age of consent, child abuse and neglect, criminal sexual...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe laws (e.g., age of consent, child abuse and neglect, criminal sexual conduct, minor consent for health care) that relate to young people’s health and the rights of adolescents to maintain their own health, and how these might influence decisions related to their health.
