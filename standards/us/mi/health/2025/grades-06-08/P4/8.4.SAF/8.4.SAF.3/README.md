# 8.4.SAF.3 - Evaluate potential options and consequences for decisions related to personal safety...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Evaluate potential options and consequences for decisions related to personal safety (e.g., dangerous weapons, digital safety, threats of violence, motor vehicle safety, physical injury) in a variety of situations.
