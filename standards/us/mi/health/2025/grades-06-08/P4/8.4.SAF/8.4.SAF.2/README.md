# 8.4.SAF.2 - Discuss the variety of systemic, environmental, and physical factors that might help...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Discuss the variety of systemic, environmental, and physical factors that might help or hinder an individual’s ability to remain safe in various situations.
