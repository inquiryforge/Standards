# 8.4.SE.6 - Articulate the benefits of abstinence, postponing sexual activity, and setting...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Articulate the benefits of abstinence, postponing sexual activity, and setting personal limits (e.g., aligning with personal or family values, understanding the changing nature of relationships, avoiding early or unintended pregnancy, reducing risk of STIs) based on individual beliefs and values.
