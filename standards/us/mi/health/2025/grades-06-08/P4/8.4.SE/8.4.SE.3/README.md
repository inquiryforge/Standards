# 8.4.SE.3 - Analyze ways to prevent pregnancy and sexually transmitted infections (STIs),...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Analyze ways to prevent pregnancy and sexually transmitted infections (STIs), including strategies that can be used before becoming sexually active (e.g., abstinence, communicating with a partner, HPV vaccine, contraception).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
