# 8.4.HR.5 - Define consent and describe factors, including drugs and alcohol, that may influence...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Define consent and describe factors, including drugs and alcohol, that may influence one’s capacity to request consent, and to give and receive consent, in a variety of situations.
