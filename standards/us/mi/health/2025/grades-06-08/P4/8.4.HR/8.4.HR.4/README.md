# 8.4.HR.4 - Describe characteristics of unhealthy and/or abusive relationships and evaluate...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe characteristics of unhealthy and/or abusive relationships and evaluate options and strategies a person might use to end those relationships, including involving a trusted adult who can help.
