# 8.4.BEPA.1 - Seek strategies to minimize barriers and maximize opportunities (e.g., time, space,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Seek strategies to minimize barriers and maximize opportunities (e.g., time, space, physical abilities, access to equipment, overuse of digital devices) that help maintain a physically active lifestyle.
