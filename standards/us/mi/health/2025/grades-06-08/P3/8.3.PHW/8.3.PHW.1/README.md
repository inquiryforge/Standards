# 8.3.PHW.1 - Determine the accessibility of products (e.g., deodorant, hair care, sunscreen,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Determine the accessibility of products (e.g., deodorant, hair care, sunscreen, dental care products), resources, and services that enhance health and identify supports and barriers to accessing the products or services.
