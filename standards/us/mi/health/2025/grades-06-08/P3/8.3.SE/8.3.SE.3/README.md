# 8.3.SE.3 - Locate valid and reliable information on puberty, personal hygiene, menstruation,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Locate valid and reliable information on puberty, personal hygiene, menstruation, and personal health products from various resources in one’s home, school, and community.
