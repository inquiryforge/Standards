# 8.3.SAF.1 - Identify sources of support, such as parents, guardians, or other trusted adults, to...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify sources of support, such as parents, guardians, or other trusted adults, to whom students can go if they or someone they know is being bullied, harassed, abused, assaulted, or exploited.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
