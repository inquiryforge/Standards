# 8.3.SAF.3 - Locate community resources that provide support and resources related to sexual...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Locate community resources that provide support and resources related to sexual exploitation or for getting help for self or others in situations related to sex trafficking.
