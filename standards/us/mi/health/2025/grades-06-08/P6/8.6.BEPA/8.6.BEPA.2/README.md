# 8.6.BEPA.2 - Analyze a variety of influences (e.g., media, peers, family, culture, stigmas,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Analyze a variety of influences (e.g., media, peers, family, culture, stigmas, school, economics, food access, food production, and cultivation) on nutrition-related beliefs and behaviors in today’s society.
