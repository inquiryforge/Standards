# 8.6.BEPA.3 - Discuss a variety of perspectives on health-promoting eating practices (e.g., food...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Discuss a variety of perspectives on health-promoting eating practices (e.g., food guidelines from other countries and cultures, cultural food practices, buying locally grown or produced foods) and describe the health benefits of and strategies for implementing these practices.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
