# 8.2.MEH.9 - Demonstrate how to respond (e.g., tell a parent, guardian, or other trusted adult,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate how to respond (e.g., tell a parent, guardian, or other trusted adult, call or text 9-8-8, OK2SAY tipline) when there is a concern about one’s own or someone else’s mental well-being or when someone is considering self-harm or suicide.
