# 8.2.MEH.3 - Identify how emotions can influence communication (e.g., anger or anxiety may affect...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify how emotions can influence communication (e.g., anger or anxiety may affect the ability to listen well) and demonstrate strategies to communicate effectively when experiencing a range of emotions and in various situations.
