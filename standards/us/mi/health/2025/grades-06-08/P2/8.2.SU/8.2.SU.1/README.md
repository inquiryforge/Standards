# 8.2.SU.1 - Analyze social situations in multiple settings (e.g., at home, at school, out with...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Analyze social situations in multiple settings (e.g., at home, at school, out with friends, at a party) that could lead to the use of nicotine, electronic vapor products, alcohol, or other illegal (age, use, or type) and potentially harmful substances.
