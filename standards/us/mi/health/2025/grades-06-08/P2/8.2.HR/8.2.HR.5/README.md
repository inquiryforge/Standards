# 8.2.HR.5 - Demonstrate effective approaches to boundary setting (e.g., acknowledging feelings,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate effective approaches to boundary setting (e.g., acknowledging feelings, communicate the boundary, target alternative) and maintenance of various boundaries (e.g., related to technology use, emotional, physical).
