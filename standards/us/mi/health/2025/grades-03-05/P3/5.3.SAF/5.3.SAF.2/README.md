# 5.3.SAF.2 - Locate trusted adults (including parents or guardians) from whom to get help if...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Locate trusted adults (including parents or guardians) from whom to get help if boundaries are being violated or one is being abused, harassed, or assaulted.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
