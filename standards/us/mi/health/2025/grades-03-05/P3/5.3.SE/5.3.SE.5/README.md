# 5.3.SE.5 - Identify parents, guardians, or other trusted adults (e.g., counselors and other...

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Identify parents, guardians, or other trusted adults (e.g., counselors and other health care professionals) whom students can ask questions about puberty, abstinence, and adolescent health issues (including abuse and neglect).
