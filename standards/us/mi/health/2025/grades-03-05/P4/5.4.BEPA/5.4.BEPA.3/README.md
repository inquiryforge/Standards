# 5.4.BEPA.3 - Discuss the benefits of balanced eating and physical activity on physical health...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Discuss the benefits of balanced eating and physical activity on physical health (e.g., supporting growth and development, ability to engage in physical activity), social health, and emotional and mental health (e.g., managing stress and emotions) when making decisions pertaining to nutrition and physical activity.
<!-- standard-text:end -->
