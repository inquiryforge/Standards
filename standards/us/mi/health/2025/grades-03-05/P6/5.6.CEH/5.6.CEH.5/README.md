# 5.6.CEH.5 - Use accurate information when discussing environmental health issues (e.g.,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Use accurate information when discussing environmental health issues (e.g., littering, deforestation, recycling, clean water) that affect people’s health.
