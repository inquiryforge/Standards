# 5.5.PHW.1 - Describe personal behaviors and strategies that promote health and/or avoid health...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Describe personal behaviors and strategies that promote health and/or avoid health risks (e.g., pedestrian safety, sun safety, protecting oneself from infectious diseases, adequate sleep, good nutrition, protective equipment, appropriate screen time, hearing protection, being physically active).
<!-- standard-text:end -->
