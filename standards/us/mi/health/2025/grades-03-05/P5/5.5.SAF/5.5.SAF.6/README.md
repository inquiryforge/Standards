# 5.5.SAF.6 - Demonstrate the ability to set and maintain developmentally appropriate boundaries...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate the ability to set and maintain developmentally appropriate boundaries (including physical, verbal, and emotional boundaries) and how to respond if those boundaries are violated.
