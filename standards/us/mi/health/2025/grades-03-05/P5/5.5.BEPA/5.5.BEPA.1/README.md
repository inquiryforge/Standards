# 5.5.BEPA.1 - Demonstrate respect for self and responsible, safe interpersonal behavior (e.g.,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate respect for self and responsible, safe interpersonal behavior (e.g., peer-to-peer, student-to-teacher) that contributes to positive social interaction in various physical activity contexts.
