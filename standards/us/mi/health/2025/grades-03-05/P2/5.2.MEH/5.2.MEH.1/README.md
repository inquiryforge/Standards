# 5.2.MEH.1 - Demonstrate an awareness that emotions may be expressed in different ways (e.g.,...

[Parent](../README.md)

## Standard

<!-- standard-text:start -->
Demonstrate an awareness that emotions may be expressed in different ways (e.g., through body language, intensity of expression) by various groups and in other cultures.
